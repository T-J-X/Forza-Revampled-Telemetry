# Phase 3 agents

| Lead | Mandate in this phase |
|---|---|
| **Agent 0 · Research Scout** (lead) | Runs the reference research and turns it into concrete techniques, not adjectives: "Taycan uses a 2-stop radial on the needle hub with a 1 px inner rim at 60% white" is the level of detail needed. |
| **Agent 3 · Design Council** (3A Glass, 3B GT7/HUD, 3C Retro/Sci-fi, 3D ADAS) | Each director owns their family template and hero concept, and argues for their family in the critique loop. |
| **Agent 1 · HMI/UX** | Enforces the rank order, glanceability under 150 ms, placement in the binnacle and hood layout, and the OSD and configurator directions. |
| **Agent 5 · Technical Artist** (consult) | Flags anything that needs a texture or a 3D asset so Phase 6 can plan it, and records it in the catalog. |

Shared bar: anything that would read as a flat rectangle, default font or generic gradient on a 4K OLED gets rejected. Every frame needs one signature moment: a material, a motion or a light behaviour.
