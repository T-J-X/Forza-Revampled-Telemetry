# Cluster catalog

Phase 3 (design system) wrote this catalog: **29 rebuilds** and **19 new next-gen cockpit concepts** across **7 families**, with one pattern-setter per family (★) and a build order. Phase 7 setup (chat 07-00) scaffolded every row, ordered the build queue, built the pilot and keeps the status column; the design-board column records each cluster's board (see [Design boards](#design-boards-penpot)). Every row obeys [docs/design/TOKENS.md](../../../docs/design/TOKENS.md), [COMPONENTS.md](../../../docs/design/COMPONENTS.md) and its family spec in [docs/design/families/](../../../docs/design/families/).

## How the catalog was made
Following the `brainstorming` discipline (lay out 2–3 approaches, pick, record why; TJX pre-approved the program, so the approval gate is already met):

| Approach | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| A. Order by family, pattern-setter first | Each family's language and helpers are proven once, then reused | Popular clusters in late families wait |
| B. Order by shared-component dependency | Fewest helper changes after the factory starts | Ignores which themes users run most |
| C. Order by expected use (defaults first) | The default cockpit and hood clusters ship early | Can strand a family with no pattern-setter |

**Chosen: A, seeded by C, grouped by B.** The two defaults lead (`LiquidGlass` is the default cockpit theme, `Gt7HoodPro` the default hood theme), the other five pattern-setters follow so every family's helpers exist before its siblings, the remaining rebuilds are grouped by the helpers they reuse, and the new concepts come last, alternating families so no family lags.

Seven Design Council directors drafted one family each (art direction, signature moment, telemetry, references, Phase 6 assets, risk), each proposing one more concept than needed; this phase kept the strongest and dropped near-duplicates:
- EdgeLitEngraving (Race Dash): same edge-lit etched-slab language as LumenEtch; one per catalog.
- EdgelitStrata (Modern Digital): same edge-lit etched-slab language as LumenEtch.
- TwinBeamCollider (Sci-Fi): its "beams meet at the shift point" signature duplicates the Modern Digital hero (Apex lock).
- VortexStreetWake (Fluid): smoke-tunnel visual duplicates AeroSmokeTunnel.
- WanderingSatellite (Classic): strong, but the family keeps its two most glanceable concepts; a candidate for a later wave.

**Honesty rules carried by every row:** telemetry comes only from the Forza Data Out packet; anything else (traffic, roads ahead) is synthesised and labelled as such. Fields not yet decoded by `fh6-core` (for example torque, fuel, lap times) are called out in the row so Phase 7 verifies them against `tests/golden/packets` first (the 97% rule).

### Liquid Glass

#### ★ LiquidGlass — build #1 · cockpit+hood
One monolithic sapphire capsule (1240×300 squircle, n 4) at tint 44, Tinted 60. It has no wallpaper: only a 1 px #FFFFFF→primary rim, a 10 px fresnel band and the crown sheen define it. Rank 3: a light pipe 14 px inside the rim runs from lower-left over the crown to lower-right, 6→14 px wide, and ends in a meniscus bead (r 20, smooth-union k 10) that lenses the rim behind it. Rank 1: 16 glass pills in a 36 px strip under the crown, plus the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 200) under a 230 px domed lens with a dimming pad. Rank 2: speed on the left, Saira 200 cap 112, ghost 8s. Rank 4: a pendant glass drop fused under the centre holds a caustic G spot. Power and boost float on the right in Saira 300.

- **Signature moment:** Upshift light-run: a specular glint starts on the new gear glyph and climbs into the dome-lens rim. There it splits and races both ways round the capsule rim at 4000 px/s, meeting in the pendant drop after about 340 ms. Meanwhile the meniscus bead slides back down the pipe on the ribbon spring, adding no lag. In a steady corner the rim crescent and crown sheen swing up to 25° round the silhouette. At the limiter only the 36 px pill strip strobes at 10 Hz; the light pipe's primary stop warms to the shift colour as a steady ramp. Showing takes a 200 ms lensing and rim ramp; hiding takes 1 frame.
- **Telemetry:** rpm (current, idle, max) with the car-profile shift and per-gear thresholds → light-pipe length, meniscus bead and the 16 pills (filling outside-in). Gear → hero glyph and upshift glint. Speed → 3-cell tabular field (0.6 hysteresis). Acceleration x/z → light azimuth (rim crescent, crown sheen, bead specular) and the caustic spot's position in the pendant drop (felt force, gBall spring). Tyre combined slip → the spot defocuses from a sharp 6 px point into a 40 px ring caustic (grip = focus). Throttle → bead core 16→22 px. Power and boost → right-flank metrics. Distance travelled → caustic phase, so the glass is still at idle. IsRaceOn → materialise. All are native fields; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Apple Liquid Glass, WWDC25 'Meet Liquid Glass' (lensing, specular travelling round the silhouette); Apple CarPlay Ultra thin-blue-bar cluster theme; Lucid Gravity Clearview floating bezel-free OLED; Mercedes Hyperscreen 'light blade in a glass lens' needle.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Optional: a caustic lens normal/refraction pair (used only inside the pendant drop, screen blend 0.32), a sapphire dome normal map for the gear lens, the shared 128² blue-noise dither tile, and a mono-white logo for an optional Michroma custom-name zone on the lower rim. MSDF atlases: Saira, Saira Condensed, Titillium Web, Michroma. Procedural fallbacks: an analytic cusp caustic and SDF dome normals.
- **Risk:** The v2 hero shows the design risk. A full-surface caustic wallpaper at about 25% luminance turned the capsule into a grey slab, the octagon gear bay borrowed the Sci-Fi motif, and the whole rim lit white at redline. To avoid it: tint ≤44 with no wallpaper, static structure ≤20% luminance, a round dome lens, and only the pill strip strobes. Performance: the capsule SDF, rims and fresnel run in one pass over the clip (about 0.4 Mpx). Our layers render once to a clipped RGBA16F target and are lensed in one tap, and 6-level bloom from the clip covers ranks 1–3 only. Estimate ≈0.28 ms, to confirm in bench-render. Hood: the scrim is 0%, so the capsule stretches to 1560×240 with a legibility halo of alpha ≤0.55.

- **Telemetry:** World position x/y/z → a dedicated preallocated 1024-node trail at 2 m spacing (2 km) with real elevation from y; speed → streak width; throttle → streak brightness; brake → warning taillight core; local velocity x/z → β and the axle-split offset; rpm → EV meter and shutter blades; gear and speed → couplet; lateral G → light azimuth on the streak's 1 px rim. - **References:** Night long-exposure light-trail photography of mountain passes; the ±3 EV, 1/3-stop exposure scale in mirrorless-camera viewfinders; GPS altitude-profile curtain charts; Ryoji Ikeda's data-light installations
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (a ribbon triangle strip built from the trail, SDF shutter blades, meter and corner marks); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600; the shared blue-noise tile to dither the streak's low-alpha tail

Auto-framing 2 km must not constantly re-zoom: step the scale per speed band with a 300 ms ease. Overlap with WindshieldHud's map ribbon is avoided by viewpoint (high orbit, past only) and by encoding (width = speed, exposure = throttle). GPU <0.06 ms.

#### TreadSeismograph — build #45 · cockpit+hood
The car writes the road. - **References:** Seismograph and polygraph strip-chart recorders; Harold Craft's 1970 stacked plots of pulsar CP 1919 (the ridgeline as a data form); MoTeC i2 suspension and kerb traces used by race engineers; Mercedes Magic Body Control road-surface scanning
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (4 ring-buffer triangle strips of 256 samples, updated in place by ring offset; SDF footprint, pods and stylus; a small fresnel glass segment); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600

#### IsochromaticLoadPath — build #36 · cockpit
A clear polycarbonate single-seater seen from above, set between crossed circular polarisers: unstressed material is optically black (off pixels), so light exists only where the car is loaded. Its front wing (1100×60, y −235) is the tach: a point-load puck rides it to the rpm position (rank 3, emissive) while a fan of fringes radiates beneath. Thirteen pills on the wing's leading edge form the shift strip (y −292). Gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200) sits in the tub at (0,−30) on a dimming pad; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 84) on the engine cover at (0,+165). Contact patches at (±400,−170) and (±400,+175) show load as monochrome isochromatics, sin²(πN) in primary only; chassis drawn in 1 px rims.

- **Signature moment:** Load transfer becomes light. Trail-brake into a corner and the outside-front contact patch blooms into 6–8 tight concentric fringe loops (Flamant circles tangent at the patch) while the unloaded inside-rear goes black. Throttle out of a hairpin lights the driven axle's patches. Lateral G rotates the analyser ±10°, so dark isoclinic bands sweep across the chassis as you turn, which is a real polariscope effect. Gear change: the wing's point load releases, and its fringe fan collapses and re-forms at the new rpm on the `snap` spring while the puck moves instantly. Limiter: the wing fringes reach order 6 and freeze in a steady `shift` tint; only the pill strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** normalized_suspension_travel FL/FR/RL/RR (decoded) plus gLat/gLong load transfer → point-load magnitude at each contact patch (`fast` spring, 2% deadband). drivetrain_type → which axle shows torque-reaction fringes under throttle (RWD rear, FWD front, AWD both), scaled by throttle. Per-wheel combined slip (packet field, decoder extension needed; verify against golden packets) and tyre slip angles → patch smear direction, with `caution`/`warning` outline steps at 0.08/0.18. rpm → puck position (`ribbon` spring with feed-forward) and wing fringe order N ∝ rpm/shiftRpm (≤6). Per-gear thresholds → pills, outside-in. gear → tub glyph. speed → tabular. gLat → analyser angle on the `light` spring.
- **References:** Photoelastic stress analysis (Brewster, 1816; Frocht, Photoelasticity, 1941); Flamant's half-plane point-load solution (1892), whose isochromatics are circles tangent at the load; engineering polariscope demonstrations of loaded discs and hooks; photoelastic granular force-chain experiments.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: a closed-form superposition of four Flamant point-load stress tensors per pixel, principal stress difference → fringe order N → sin²(πN). This is art-correct, not FEA. Optional car-class plan silhouettes as SVG through resvg (single-seater, GT, SUV, truck) with an SDF capsule-and-box fallback. 128² blue-noise for low-alpha fringe tails. Exo 2 MSDF.
- **Risk:** Fringe moiré and shimmer on the WRGB subpixels at high N: cap N at 8, antialias each fringe with fwidth, and fade fringes thinner than 3 px. Noisy suspension data would make the loops boil; the spring and deadband keep idle still. A plan-view car can read as a toy, so draw it with 1 px rims only, at ≤20% luminance, with no fills. Perf: four tensor sums per pixel over two clips (about 0.25 MP), one pass, about 0.03 ms, the cheapest live visual in the family.

#### HarmonographDrive — build #47 · cockpit
A pendulum drawing machine that reads the drivetrain. Two mirrored 90° arcs bracket the gear like parentheses (R 300 around (0,−60)) and fill bottom-to-top together with rpm (rank 3), Barlow Semi Condensed numerals outboard. Gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200) at (0,−60); speed (Exo 2 300, cap 112) at (0,+95). Fifteen Ø22 pendulum bobs form the shift strip at y −300. Under the speed, a 640×80 drawing table carries a 2 px harmonograph figure in primary whose frequency ratio is the current gear's learned overall ratio, so every gear draws its own figure; the stroke decays along its length like ink running dry. Arcs are G-lit steel channels; Michroma wordmark between the arc feet.

- **Signature moment:** Grip is stillness. With the drivetrain locked, the figure is frozen. The instant the driven wheels spin, it precesses (rotates through its own lobes) at a rate proportional to the slip. It reverses direction under brake lock-up and smears visibly during clutch slip, so traction reads as motion in the corner of the eye. Each gear owns a figure (1st a dense 5-lobe rosette, top gear a near-ellipse). On a shift the pen redraws the new figure over 200 ms while the glyph swaps in frame 1. Limiter: the arcs are full and the figure tints steady `shift`; only the bob strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** rpm and driven-wheel rotation speeds (packet fields, decoder extension needed; verify offsets on tests/golden/packets per the 97% rule). drivetrain_type selects the driven axle; the live ratio is r = ω_engine/ω_wheel. A per-gear ratio is learned when /r − r̄/ < 1% for 0.5 s with clutch at 0, and stored in the car profile. Frequencies (1, map(r_gear) into 1.5–5) give the figure; phase precession dφ/dt = k·(r/r_gear − 1) with a 1.5% deadband. throttle → damping (lift = decaying spiral, full = crisp closed figure). rpm → parenthesis arcs (`ribbon` spring with feed-forward). Per-gear thresholds → bobs. gear → figure swap. speed → tabular. The ratio is synthesised from these fields; the game sends no gear-ratio data.
- **References:** Blackburn-pendulum harmonographs (19th-century drawing machines); rose-engine guilloché geometry from watch-dial finishing; X-Y oscilloscope vector art; a chassis-dyno printout's per-gear ratio curves.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: a static 4096-vertex polyline whose positions are evaluated in the vertex shader from 6 uniforms, drawn as an SDF-antialiased 2 px stroke with a length-wise alpha falloff. 128² blue-noise. Exo 2, Barlow Semi Condensed and Michroma MSDF. Brand wordmark (crest optional at ≤96 px).
- **Risk:** It depends on wheel rotation speeds the decoder does not expose yet, and on per-car ratio learning. Until a gear is learned, the figure uses rpm/speed with a nominal 0.33 m tyre radius and shows no precession, so it can never fake slip. Precession must stay subtle at cruise, or the figure jitters on bumps: use the deadband plus the `fast` spring on r. The figure is rank 4 and never blooms. Perf: one 4k-vertex line draw plus two arc SDFs, about 0.03 ms.
