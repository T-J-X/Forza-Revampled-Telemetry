**Category note.** The light trail is a volume floating in space, seen from a fixed camera; no glass panes.

A long-exposure photograph whose shutter never closes. A fixed high three-quarter camera (pitch 38°) looks down on black, and the last 2 km of your path glows as a 3D light streak with true elevation: width ∝ speed, brightness ∝ throttle, and a warning taillight core wherever you braked. It fades by distance driven, never by time. The car is the streak's bright head. Z1: a 40 px shutter strip of nine 64 px blade pods, filling outside-in. Below it sits the rank-3 exposure-meter tach: 1/3-stop ticks from −3 to +3 EV, the shift rpm at 0 EV, the redline as overexposure, and a glowing cursor. Gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170) and speed (Saira Light, cap 112) form a centred couplet over the image, inside 1 px viewfinder corner marks (rank 5).

- **Signature moment:** The axle split. In a drift the single streak splits into front- and rear-axle trails, offset by wheelbase·sin β, and a faint caution veil fills the gap between them, so the drift angle is written into the road as a widening ribbon. When grip returns the trails re-merge on the fast spring. At the shift rpm the meter cursor lands on 0 EV and the shutter blades close inward in the shift colour. At the limiter only the shutter strip strobes 10 Hz.
- **Telemetry:** World position x/y/z → a dedicated preallocated 1024-node trail at 2 m spacing (2 km) with real elevation from y; speed → streak width; throttle → streak brightness; brake → warning taillight core; local velocity x/z → β and the axle-split offset; rpm → EV meter and shutter blades; gear and speed → couplet; lateral G → light azimuth on the streak's 1 px rim. No map exists: only roads you have driven appear, and the fade is by distance, so idle is still.
- **References:** Night long-exposure light-trail photography of mountain passes; the ±3 EV, 1/3-stop exposure scale in mirrorless-camera viewfinders; GPS altitude-profile curtain charts; Ryoji Ikeda's data-light installations
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (a ribbon triangle strip built from the trail, SDF shutter blades, meter and corner marks); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600; the shared blue-noise tile to dither the streak's low-alpha tail
- **Risk:** Self-overlapping streaks can blow out into a white blob and break the rank order. The streak is rank 4: cap alpha at ≤160, use max-blend rather than additive where it crosses itself, and never write it to emissive; only the cursor, blades and gear glow. Auto-framing 2 km must not constantly re-zoom: step the scale per speed band with a 300 ms ease. Overlap with WindshieldHud's map ribbon is avoided by viewpoint (high orbit, past only) and by encoding (width = speed, exposure = throttle). GPU <0.06 ms.
