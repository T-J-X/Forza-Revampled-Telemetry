# Vernier Loupe (`VernierLoupe`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 31 (VernierLoupe — build #30 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 30

Frameless and horizontal. A 1200 px vernier rail runs across the crown with ticks every 100 rpm (2 px minor, 5 px major) and Barlow Semi Condensed 500 numerals at cap 20, all ≤20% luminance, plus a 4 px primary fill from the left. The precise reading is a 170×110 glass loupe that glides to the current rpm and magnifies the scale ×2.4 by self-lensing, with the lensed ticks lit in primary. Rank 1: 13 round cabochon pods in a 38 px strip above the rail. Below: the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 200) in a fixed 230 px lens, and the speed on the left in Saira 200 cap 112, ghost 8s. Rank 4: a 120 px G-lens roving over a 24 px dot lattice under the gear. Optional Michroma logo zone at the rail's left end.

- **Signature moment:** Lens whip: as rpm falls on an upshift, the loupe slides back on the ribbon spring (feed-forward, no lag). The ticks inside it smear radially with a 3-tap blur scaled by the loupe's velocity, then snap sharp when it stops, and a glint runs once round its rim. In the last 300 rpm before the shift point, magnification swells from ×2.4 to ×3.2, so the final ticks visibly grow. At the limiter only the cabochon strip strobes at 10 Hz; the loupe rim holds a steady ring in the shift colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, max rpm and the car profile (shift rpm, redline, peak-torque → peak-power operating band) → rail fill, loupe position and magnification, the 13 pods, and a faint operating-band tint on the rail. Gear and speed → the fixed lens and the tabular field. Acceleration x/z → the G-lens position over the dot lattice (felt force); the dots under it are magnified, so load reads as dot size. Tyre combined slip → G-lens rim steps from neutral to caution to warning. Lateral G → light azimuth on all three lens rims. Native fields only; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Watchmaker's loupe and vernier caliper scales; the iOS text-selection magnifier as precedent for a lens that is the readout; Porsche 550 Spyder tachometer operating band; Oblivion's dot lattice (GMUNK).
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: SDF rail and ticks, a closed-form magnifier UV warp and the dot lattice. Optional: the shared sapphire dome normal map for the lenses, and a mono-white brand logo for the logo zone. MSDF atlases: Saira, Saira Condensed, Barlow Semi Condensed, Titillium Web, Michroma.
- **Risk:** Design risk: a sliding magnifier is slower to read in peripheral vision than a filled bar, and lensing a hairline scale can shimmer. To avoid it: the 4 px fill ribbon carries the coarse reading and the loupe adds precision. The scale and lattice render once (again on a scale change) into clipped, mip-mapped targets, so the warp samples filtered texels. Performance: three one-tap lens warps plus bloom on ranks 1–3 only. Estimate ≈0.18 ms. Hood: the rail widens to 1500 px with a legibility halo of alpha ≤0.55.
