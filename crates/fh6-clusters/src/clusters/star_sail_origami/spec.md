**Category note.** Placed here by the category review of 2026-11-28: the sail's panels tilt at real depths behind a glass capsule, with parallax, in both views. The capsule is the kit's clear optical glass, and the fold follows its row in [Real geometry and maths](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md#real-geometry-and-maths). Its family stays Sci-Fi.

A deployable light-sail. Behind the gear, a pleated parallelogram-fold sheet (2 rows x 24 panels) extends symmetrically from a 180 px folded stack to 1360 px wide. Its span is the rank-3 tach, over a hairline rpm scale. Each facet is a 1 px-rimmed quad shaded only by the G-driven light, so panels swap brightness as they open (fill at 35% luminance or less, rims up to 70% white). Rank 1: Saira 600 gear (cap 200) on the spacecraft bus, a squircle glass capsule (alpha 44, 1 px rim, crescent), under a 36 px 16-pod strip. Rank 2: Saira 300 tabular speed (cap 96) under the bus. Rank 4: an unglowing four-quadrant sun-sensor G meter under the speed.

- **Signature moment:** Light from geometry, then deploy lock. As rpm rises the folds open, and each facet flips from dark to lit as its normal crosses the light vector; in a corner the moving light makes the highlights roll across the sail. At the shift rpm the sail reaches full span and the fold angle goes flat. All 48 facets then face the light together, and one steady specular sheet in the shift colour crosses the sail while only the strip strobes at 10 Hz. Upshift: the sail retracts with the rpm on the ribbon spring, rippling dark-to-light inward. Boot: the bus capsule materialises in 200 ms, then the sail unfolds to live rpm.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm sets the sail span and fold angle (rpm / shift rpm -> 0..90 deg, ribbon spring with feed-forward) and the strip. Gear drives the glyph and Speed the speed. Felt G (-AccelerationX/Z) sets the light vector (azimuth 135 + 25 x gLat / 1.5 g) that shades every facet, and places the sun-sensor spot with a 500 ms trail. Pitch and Roll tilt the sail's parallax layer by up to 3 px. Boost drives the bus crescent intensity.
- **References:** Origami-folded deployable solar arrays and the IKAROS solar-sail demonstrator (Miura-ori fold); spacecraft quadrant sun sensors; Apple Liquid Glass 'highlights follow geometry' (WWDC25); the dauphine facet swap from the research's horology section
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: 48 quads with analytic normals. An optional 1K brushed-film anisotropic normal map for facet sheen (procedural anisotropic fallback). Saira MSDF atlas.
- **Risk:** A wide reflective sheet could break the colour budget and make rank 3 glow everywhere. Facet fill stays at 35% luminance or less, only the sail's two outer edges and the leading lit facet are emissive, and panels stay within glass alpha 64. Span and fold come straight from rpm through the ribbon spring with feed-forward, so the sail never lags. Cost: 48 quads plus the bus glass, about 0.1 ms.
