**The race engineer's screen as a dash** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "some more sim like"). Where `SimInputTrace` shows what the driver does, this dash shows what the car does, in the look of the analysis screens an engineer watches on the pit wall: a g-g diagram, a power curve and a table of channel maxima. Step 1 researches the real tools (MoTeC i2, Cosworth Pi Toolbox, McLaren ATLAS and the telemetry pages of the sims) and names the one this dash is benchmarked against.

Starting layout, on a strict grid with tabular figures and no ornament. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 200) at (0, −60), under a row of 14 shift LEDs (760 × 32). Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) under the gear. Rank 3: the rev bar across the top, drawn over the car's own power curve, which the dash learns as it is driven: the bar's fill shows the rpm, and the curve behind it shows where the power peaks. Rank 4, left: a Ø300 g-g diagram with rings at 0.5 g, the live dot with a 500 ms trail, and the envelope the driver has reached, held in 24 sectors. Rank 4, right: a channel table of six rows, each a label, a live value and a held maximum: lateral G left and right, braking G, acceleration G, top speed and peak power. Rank 5: unit labels and the logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** A bigger envelope. When the live dot pushes past the stored envelope in any sector, that edge moves out with it and holds, the new edge stays highlighted for 1.5 s in a steady `accent`, and a small tag shows the gain, for example `+0.04 g`. The driver sees the car's limits grow corner by corner. On an upshift a marker drops on the rev bar from the rpm before the shift to the rpm after it, on top of the power curve, so it is plain whether the next gear lands in the power. On the limiter only the LED row strobes.
- **Telemetry:** acceleration → g-g dot, the 24-sector envelope (a fixed `[f32; 24]`) and the table's four G rows; rpm and power → the learned power curve (peak power per 250 rpm band in a fixed `[f32; 48]`, cleared when the car ordinal changes); rpm, idle and max with per-gear thresholds → rev bar, LED row and limiter; gear → numeral and the shift marker; speed → tabular speed and the top-speed row; boost → an optional seventh row. Every channel is a verified field. Tyre, suspension and temperature channels, which a real pit wall shows, are not verified: they are left out, and four friction circles are added only after the real-game check.
- **References:** MoTeC i2 g-g and histogram views; Cosworth Pi Toolbox; McLaren ATLAS; the engineer's page of endurance and Formula 1 timing screens; `SimInputTrace` and `BoschDdu` in this catalog
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the LED row in a machined carrier, and thin anodised frame edges for the diagram and the table with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: the anti-glare matrix of the display face and a carbon surface for the carrier. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800, Chakra Petch 500 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** An analysis screen is dense by nature and the rank order can drown: gear, speed and the LED row are the only things at full light, the diagram and the table stay under 35% luminance, and the zen tier hides the table. A learned power curve is empty for the first pull: draw the plain rev bar until three bands hold data, and never show a curve from another car. Held maxima must not be read as live values: live values are tabular and bright, held ones are small, dim and marked `MAX`. Perf: the curve is 48 points in chunks of 8, the envelope 24 points, est. 0.2 ms.
