A split-seconds chronograph opened into a sector. Two long lance hands rise from a hidden pivot 640 px below the anchor and sweep a 110° fan whose chapter arc (R 880) spans the binnacle, giving about 15 px per 100 rpm. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 210, in a sapphire lens (alpha 44, 1 px rim, crescent) on X = 1920 that the hands pass behind; 15 lume batons lie on a straight chord above the arc, 30 × 760 px. Rank 2: tabular Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 100, in the fan's right wing, which no hand reaches. Rank 3: a primary-colour main hand plus an accent hairline split hand. Idle→shift spans −55°→0°, and the top 15% of the range opens out to +20°. Rank 4: a 'shot group' of the last five shifts under the speed. Rank 5: a titanium arc rail and the Michroma name. Hood view: the same sector at R 1000 inside the 1760 × 400 envelope.

- **Signature moment:** Catch-up. On every upshift the split hand freezes at the rpm where the gear changed. The arc between it and the 12 o'clock index fills in three steps: go within ±100 rpm, caution within ±300, warning beyond. The arc's length carries the same message, so colour is never the only cue. After 1.2 s the split hand catches up to the main hand on the fast spring. It is a shift coach for a manual-gearbox driver, built from a horology complication. At the limiter only the baton chord strobes, and the main hand's lume core burns a steady shift colour. At idle both hands sit superimposed and still.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the main hand (needle spring with feed-forward) and the baton thresholds. A gear-increment edge, with the rpm from the frame before the drop, captures the split hand; the clutch byte confirms a real shift. Downshifts are optional: they capture the blip peak against a rev-match target from per-gear rpm/speed ratios learned live into a fixed [f32; 10] table. The car-profile shift rpm sets 12 o'clock (fallback: limiter × 0.97). speed drives the tabular readout. The last five shift deltas live in a fixed [f32; 5] ring buffer for the shot group. Lateral G drives the light on the rail and the hand ridges. All of these fields are already decoded.
- **References:** Split-seconds (rattrapante) chronographs; 1930s sector ('fan') speedometers and pressure gauges; Porsche 992.2 shift-at-12 rev counter; AiM/MoTeC per-gear shift thresholds
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: SDF hands, arc, batons and lens; MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed, Barlow Semi Condensed and Michroma. An optional brushed-titanium tangential map can be added for the arc rail.
- **Risk:** Hands about 900 px long sweep across the game scene and can cut through the road view. Clip them to the envelope, fade the roots behind the gear lens, keep them ≤14 px wide, and end the sector at ±55° so the tips stay inside 1440 px (1760 px on the hood). The coach must never be mistaken for the live reading: the split hand is a 2 px accent hairline with no bloom. Performance: SDF only, est. 0.12 ms.
