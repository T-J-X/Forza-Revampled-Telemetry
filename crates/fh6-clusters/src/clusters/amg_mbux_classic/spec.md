**Real display first** (TJX, 2026-11-28: each AMG MBUX version "as close as possible"). This dash is the **Classic** menu of the second-generation MBUX driver display on AMG cars (AMG GT C192, SL R232, C 43 / C 63, CLE 53). What the sources say: "The Classic menu shows the driver display separated into speedometer and tachometer"; the press calls it "the proven display with two tubes and changing content between them"; the manual's legend lists speedometer, tachometer, gear display, a temperature or battery gauge, outside temperature, the centre display content, the time and the fuel level; a review of the SL 63 describes "two analogue (but digital) dials, speed and fuel on the left, and then rpm and oil temp on the right", and another notes that both dials have "a red digitized needle". On AMG cars the centre content can be engine data (current power and torque), set-up or temperatures. **Take from frame grabs in step 1:** the AMG dial artwork, both scale ends and the red area, where the small gauges sit, the needle shape and the typeface.

Starting layout: two Ø430 tubes at (−400, −30) and (+400, −30), speedometer left and rev counter right, each a rendered dial under a glass lens with a red light needle (rank 3), and nothing joining them. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 180) between the tubes at (0, −60) with the yellow `M` tag, under an upshift bar of 12 cells (760 × 30) that bridges the two dial crowns and fills outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira 300, cap 96) inside the speedometer, 400 px from the gear. Rank 4: the centre content under the gear, 360 × 150. Rank 5: the small inset gauges, the labels and the AMG logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** The light needles. Each needle is a blade of light inside the lens: it lights the ticks it passes and leaves a short afterglow on the scale, so a hard pull paints a red sweep that fades in 200 ms. At the shift rpm the rev counter's red area holds a steady `shift` glow and the upshift bar closes on its centre; on the limiter only the bar strobes. On an upshift the rev needle drops on the `needle` spring with feed-forward and the afterglow shows the size of the drop.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → rev counter, upshift bar and limiter; speed → speedometer needle and the tabular readout; gear → centre numeral with the `M` tag; power and derived torque (power ÷ angular engine speed) → the engine-data centre content; acceleration → a G-force centre content; boost → the rev counter's inset gauge. Fuel, coolant and oil temperature, outside temperature and the clock have no verified telemetry: their places hold boost and throttle, or the field is verified first (97% rule). The centre content is the driver's choice in the OSD (`AmgMbuxClassicCentre`).
- **References:** MBUSA owner's-manual supplement for the AMG C-Class (the Classic figure and its nine-item legend); Mercedes press text for the C-Class W206; reviews of the SL 63 and GLC driver displays; owner videos of the AMG GT (C192) cluster for frame grabs; `PorscheAnalog` in this catalog for how rendered dials are built
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the two dial tubes (ring, face, lens) with three light azimuths, and the needle blade. ComfyUI: the AMG dial-face material the frame grabs show. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 800, Saira 300, Barlow Semi Condensed 500 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** Two equal dials pull the eye from the gear: the gear numeral is the brightest thing on screen and the dial faces stay under 20% luminance. Twin round dials are also what `PorscheAnalog` and `PaganiHorologerie` use, so this one must read as a Mercedes screen, not as hardware: follow the frame grabs for the lens, the needle and the type. The red needles and red area are steady, never flashing, and the scale numerals carry the reading without colour. Perf: two dial plates and two needles, est. 0.15 ms.
