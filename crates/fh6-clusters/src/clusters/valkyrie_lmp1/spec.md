- **Family:** Race Dash (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/race-dash.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/race-dash.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/race-dash/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/race-dash/)

**Category note.** The Aston Martin Valkyrie's own display leads. Where that is a screen on the steering wheel, step 1's rule applies: take its parts, not its shape. The Porsche 963 and McLaren references in this brief are benchmarks only. The Race Dash asset rule applies.

A hypercar endurance wheel face, not a panel: a 1440×420 forged-carbon yoke (chopped-fibre marble, unlike Bosch's twill) with angled shoulders around a 1000×340 true-black window. Rank 1: a straight 15-LED crown (34 px) across the window top and a `race` 800 gear at cap 250 in a tall portrait well. Rank 3 is the well's frame: an 8 px rpm channel climbing the left side, crossing the top, descending the right. Rank 2: speed, cap 112, left of the well; a signed lap delta at cap 84 right. Rank 4: a 4 s throttle/brake/steer engineer trace under the gear. Shoulders hold knurled anodised encoder rings with intervention arcs. Labels Titillium Web; delta and sectors in B612 Mono.

- **Signature moment:** Predictive crown: below the first per-gear threshold the 15 LEDs run at 40% intensity as a lap-delta meter, growing from the centre pod rightward in `go` when gaining and leftward in `warning` when losing (1 LED = 0.1 s, clamped ±0.7 s). The frame rpm crosses stage 1, the crown hard-switches to outside-in shift mode at full intensity. At the limiter only the crown strip strobes at 10 Hz while the gear-well perimeter tach holds steady in `shift`, the Valkyrie's one never-flashing cue.
- **Telemetry:** rpm vs car-profile per-gear thresholds → crown and perimeter tach; gear → well glyph; speed_ms → speed; distance traveled minus lap-start distance compared against a recorded best-lap time-by-distance table (fixed 2048 bins, no allocation; synthesised, hidden in free roam and until one clean lap exists) → delta crown and readout; lap number, current/last/best lap → sector strip using synthesised thirds of the recorded lap length; accel/brake/steer → engineer trace (go / warning / neutral hairline); per-wheel slip ratio + drivetrain_type + throttle/brake → synthesised TC (outer) and ABS (inner) arcs on the left encoder; boost_psi → right encoder outer arc, fuel → inner arc; tyre temps ×4 → corner tags in thermal steps; acceleration_x → light and parallax.
- **References:** Porsche 963 LMDh wheel (top shift/warning lights, messages, lap times); AiM MXG Strada predictive lap-delta LED mode; MoTeC i2-style throttle/brake overlay traces; McLaren MCL40 2026 wheel (signed bar that vanishes at idle)
- **Phase 6 assets:** Forged-carbon marble albedo+normal (1024², seamless, asset-forge recipe + seed) with a procedural Voronoi-chip fallback; knurled encoder-ring normal strip 2048×64 with an SDF sawtooth fallback; MSDF Saira Condensed 700/800, Titillium Web 600, B612 Mono.
- **Risk:** Design: delta-mode LEDs mistaken for shift lights. Avoid: 40% intensity, centre-out pattern only, a same-frame switch at stage 1 with 120 rpm hysteresis, an OSD toggle, and a dark crown in free roam. Perf: 240-point trace from a fixed ring buffer as one SDF strip ≈0.02 ms; forged carbon one textured pass ≈0.03 ms; bloom on crown, well tach and gear ≈0.12 ms; ≈0.22 ms total. Hood [4]: shoulders spread to the 1760×400 envelope and the trace shortens to 3 s. Needs distance, lap and slip-ratio fields added to the fh6-core decoder.
