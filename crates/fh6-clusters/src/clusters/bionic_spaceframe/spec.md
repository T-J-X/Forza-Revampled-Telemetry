- **Family:** Race Dash (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/race-dash.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/race-dash.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/race-dash/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/race-dash/)

**Category note.** Inspired by the Czinger 21C's printed node chassis: the frame is the dash. The 21C is not in the game.

No panel, no bezel ring: the cluster hangs in a generative titanium spaceframe grown along stress lines from two column mounts (±420, +210) to a top beam and a central hub ring. At most 48 brushed struts (tangent-aligned anisotropic GGX, base ≤12% luminance) hold every module in a machined node boss: 10 pods in beam nodes, the `race` 800 gear at cap 240 inside the hub, speed (`race` 700, cap 112) in a left sub-node, corner-ticked lap tiles between right struts, four tyre nodes where the lower struts meet. Rank 3 is a 12 px LED channel milled along the beam. One `light` slides highlights along every strut through a corner; rims 1 px; only ranks 1–3 glow.

- **Signature moment:** Load path: on each upshift a specular glint (non-emissive, 60% white peak) leaves the hub ring and runs down the strut chains to both column mounts at 4000 px/s, as if the shift torque flowed through the frame into the car; on a downshift it runs mount to hub. At the limiter the 10 pods strobe inside the 36 px beam strip while their node bosses hold a steady `shift` reflection. Idle is still: constant G means frozen highlights.
- **Telemetry:** acceleration_x → light azimuth (strut highlights) and ±4 px frame parallax; rpm + car-profile thresholds → pods, strobe and beam LED channel; gear up/down edge → load-path glint direction; speed_ms → speed; tyre temps ×4 → wheel nodes in thermal steps, combined slip → their 1 px slip outline; lap fields → right tiles (free roam: power_watts, boost_psi, distance); LIM, FUEL and synthesised TC/ABS → two lamps per side node.
- **References:** Czinger 21C additively manufactured node chassis; topology-optimised (generative-design) aerospace brackets; Bugatti Tourbillon skeleton stack (depth without boxes); Apple Liquid Glass highlights that travel with geometry
- **Phase 6 assets:** Seeded lattice from tools/asset-forge (Blender geometry-nodes stress-line recipe) as a ≤20k-tri glTF plus a baked 1440×560 RG16F distance+tangent field; brushed-titanium anisotropy/normal tile; procedural fallback: ≤48 SDF capsules from the same seed, baked to the same field at load.
- **Risk:** Design: the lattice turns into greeble and pulls the eye from ranks 1–3. Avoid: every strut must carry a module (stress-line recipe, ≤48 struts), static luminance ≤12%, the zen tier fades everything but beam and hub. Perf: one pass over the envelope sampling the baked field plus anisotropic specular ≈0.03 ms; the glint is a 1D parameter along precomputed strut arc length (no extra pass); bloom on beam, pods and gear ≈0.12 ms; ≈0.2 ms total.
