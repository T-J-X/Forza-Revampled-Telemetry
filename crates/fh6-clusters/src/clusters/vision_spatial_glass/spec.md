# Vision Spatial Glass (`VisionSpatialGlass`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 2 (VisionSpatialGlass — build #8 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 8

A frameless spatial triptych at four depths. Front: a 480×330 squircle pane (tint 36) holding the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 180) above the speed (Saira 200, cap 96, ghost 8s). Behind it, two 420×200 wing panes are toed in 14° by a true homography, so their far edges are 12% shorter. Rank 3: a 10 px light ribbon crosses both wings left to right and passes behind the front pane, where it is visibly refracted (14 px offset, 1.5 px chromatic split at the rim). Rank 1: a 900×38 glass blade floats frontmost above the stack, with 15 pods filling outside-in. Rank 4: a hovering attitude puck under the front pane. The wings carry only Titillium Web nano labels. Every rim takes the one G-driven light.

- **Signature moment:** Z-hop upshift: a 1-frame 60% glint lights on the far wing's rim and hops forward one layer at a time (wing, front pane, shift blade), 40 ms per layer. At the same time the front pane recedes 6 px in depth on the fast spring and the new gear pops forward from 1.12. Lateral G shears the stack by 0/2/4/6 px per g (parallax spring ω 12 rad/s), so depth is something you read, not something drawn. At the limiter only the 38 px blade strobes at 10 Hz; the wings' outer rims hold a steady shift-colour line. On show, the panes materialise back to front over 200 ms.
- **Telemetry:** rpm + car-profile thresholds → wing ribbon length and the 15 blade pods. Gear and speed → front pane. Acceleration x/z (felt force) → layer parallax, the one light's azimuth on every rim, and the bead rolling on the puck. Pitch and roll → puck tilt drawn as a perspective ellipse. Verify both fields against tests/golden/packets before use (97% rule); the fallback is tilt from smoothed G. Yaw rate → wing toe-in ±2°. Brake → caution step on the puck rim. Tyre combined slip → the bead's halo spreads. Native fields only; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** visionOS window depth stacking with ornaments floating in front; Apple Liquid Glass lensing (WWDC25); BMW Panoramic iDrive upper-layer/lower-layer choreography; Porsche Taycan curved cluster with angled side zones.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: SDF panes and blade, homography-projected wings and an analytic puck. It shares the LiquidGlass sapphire normal map (optional) and the blue-noise tile. MSDF atlases: Saira, Saira Condensed, Titillium Web.
- **Risk:** Design risk: four translucent layers overlapping into a muddy grey stack (the v1 build read as three unrelated boxes), and digits on tilted panes failing the blur gate. To avoid it: every digit sits on the untilted front pane, the wings carry only the ribbon and nano labels, pane overlap is ≤12% of the area, and summed glass alpha is clamped to 64 where panes overlap. Performance: the refraction pass samples only the ribbon target inside the 480×330 front-pane footprint, the panes are 4 SDF draws over about 0.5 Mpx, and bloom covers ranks 1–3 only. Estimate ≈0.30 ms.
