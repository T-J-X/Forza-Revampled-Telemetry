**Category note.** In the Liquid Glass category the prism is clear `OpticalGlass`, and the colour split is the prism deviation of `docs/specs/optics.md` section 10, with body slip turning the prism (yaw is not verified). Tint comes from absorption only. The neon of this brief lives in the beam and in the wet-road mirror, never as tubes on the glass. Its family stays Sci-Fi.

A rain-night optics bench. A 280 px equilateral glass prism, point-up at X=1920 (tint alpha 44, 1 px rim, fresnel band, neon-tube edges), holds the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 180) on a dimming pad. A white beam enters from the left, with the speed (Saira Condensed 700, cap 104) above it. Rank 3: from the right face a three-band fan in primary, secondary and accent exits; its length and opening grow with rpm against a 1 px rpm ruler. Rank 1: a 36 px 15-pod strip across the crown. Rank 4: under the prism, four short unglowing tyre beams that split with each tyre's slip. Rank 5: a wet-road mirror appears only when puddles are present.

- **Signature moment:** Dispersion is slip. With grip, the three bands are drawn coincident as one ink-white ray. As the rear slip angle grows they split by up to 18 px per band, so a drift literally breaks the light into colour. Aberration is driven by telemetry and is never an always-on effect. At the shift rpm the fan reaches the end of the ruler and takes the shift colour, steady, while only the strip strobes at 10 Hz. When WheelInPuddleDepth > 0, the mirror fades in over 200 ms, rippled by SurfaceRumble.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm drives the fan length and opening and the strip. Gear drives the prism glyph and Speed the speed. Accel sets the incoming beam's brightness. Rear TireSlipAngle and AngularVelocityY set the band separation (yaw x 6 + beta x 18 px), which is 0 with grip. TireCombinedSlip x4 splits the four mini beams. WheelInPuddleDepth x4 drives the mirror opacity and SurfaceRumble x4 its ripple amplitude. Felt lateral G sets the light azimuth for the prism rim and fresnel band.
- **References:** The white-light prism dispersion experiment; night photography of Shibuya and Shinjuku neon on wet asphalt; Blade Runner 2049's 'degradation as register' (Territory Studio); Cyberpunk 2077's glitch-as-event rule
- **Phase 6 assets:** An optional 2K wet-asphalt normal map for the mirror ripple (procedural sine-ripple fallback). A prism thickness/normal map (analytic fallback). Saira Condensed MSDF atlas. Neon tubes and bands are procedural.
- **Risk:** Chromatic splitting could smear the digits, and a multicolour fan could break the colour budget. Splitting applies only to the fan and the mini beams, never to digits, and snaps back to 0 within a frame at grip. Bands sit at 70% alpha, white at 15% of lit area or less, and warning red is never used. Cost: prism self-lensing over a 300 px region, 3 bands, and a mirror built as a flipped, blurred re-draw of the emissive buffer clipped to 1400x120, about 0.25 ms; the mirror is off when dry.
