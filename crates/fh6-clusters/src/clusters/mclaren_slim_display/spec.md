# McLaren Slim Display (`MclarenSlimDisplay`): brief

- **Category:** Supercar · **Target:** `inspired` · **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit+hood
- **Family:** Modern Digital 2027 (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/modern-digital.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/modern-digital.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/)

**Inspired by the McLaren Folding Driver Display** of the 720S and 765LT (in the game as the 2021 McLaren 765LT). Recalled from McLaren's press material and to confirm in step 1: in Full Display Mode the screen stands upright behind the wheel with a full instrument layout that changes with the Comfort, Sport and Track settings; in Slim Display Mode the whole screen folds down into the dash and leaves a narrow strip with only the speed, the gear and the engine revs as a linear bar, to clear the view on track. This dash maps the two modes onto the two views: the full display is the cockpit view, and the slim strip is the hood view, where a clear road matters most.

Starting layout. Cockpit, the full display as a floating 900 × 330 slab in a thin housing (the real display is a physical body, so this dash may have one): rank 1, the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 200) at centre under a straight shift strip of 12 cells (760 × 30) that fills outside-in; rank 2, tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) beside the gear; rank 3, a horizontal rev bar across the slab's top, numbered from `EngineMaxRpm`; rank 4, G and pedals at the left, power and boost at the right. Hood, the slim strip: one 1500 × 86 bar with the rev bar running its full length, the gear in a raised centre tab (cap 150) and the speed beside it, and nothing else.

- **Signature moment:** The fold. When the dash appears, or the driver picks slim in the OSD, the slab pitches down about its lower edge as a rigid body (a true homography, foreshortened by cos θ and shaded by the light) while the strip's content rises out of its top edge, 600 ms on the `default` spring; the reverse unfolds it. Gear, speed and revs stay readable through the whole move because they are the only things that exist in both states. On the limiter only the shift strip strobes; in the slim strip the last tenth of the rev bar holds a steady `shift` colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → rev bar, shift strip and limiter; gear → centre numeral; speed → tabular speed; acceleration → G dot; throttle and brake → pedal bars; power and boost → right-hand readouts; lateral G → the light on the housing's edges. The fold follows the view or the driver's setting (`config.extra` key `MclarenSlimDisplayMode`), never a guessed track mode.
- **References:** McLaren 720S and 765LT Folding Driver Display in Full and Slim Display Mode, from McLaren's press images and owner videos of the fold; McLaren Senna and 750S driver displays for the later graphics; `Gt7HoodPro` in this catalog as the hood-view benchmark
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the display body (satin-black housing, glass face, the hinge edge) rendered at five fold angles with matching normal maps, and the slim strip's top edge. ComfyUI: the anti-glare matrix of the screen face and an Alcantara surface for the dash top that the slab folds into. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** The fold is a large moving object beside the road view: it runs only on a view change or a setting change, never while driving at speed by itself. The slim strip is 86 px tall and sits over a bright road with the scrim at 0%: the gear tab and the speed need a feathered dimming pad and must pass the blur gate there. The exact strip content, the rev bar's numbering and the colours come from the frames, not from this brief. Perf: one slab plate and one bar, est. 0.12 ms.
