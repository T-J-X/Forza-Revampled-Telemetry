- **Category:** Abstract · **Target:** `concept` · **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit
- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)

**A flower of clear glass petals, one petal for each gear.** The figure is taken from the breathing flower that Apple shows in the Mindfulness app of Apple Vision Pro and in the Breathe animation of Apple Watch: equal translucent circles that open from one disc into a rosette and close again. That is recalled, not checked, because the search budget had run out: step 1 confirms it from Apple's own pages and measures the real animation (how many circles, how far they open, how they overlap). Here the revs do the breathing, and the flower has as many petals as the gear that is engaged.

Starting layout. Rank 4, the flower: N equal discs of clear optical glass, radius R = 110, whose centres sit on a circle of radius d round (0, −10), at the angles 90° + 360° × i ÷ N. N is the gear. The even petals sit one depth behind the odd ones. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 150) on a true-black core, Ø190, in front of the flower's middle, and ten clear beads on a straight line at y −296 (760 × 28) as the shift lights, filling outside-in. Rank 3: a thin arc of radius 246 round the flower, 240° over the top from 8 o'clock to 4 o'clock, lit from the left, numerals outside it. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira 300, cap 90) on true black to the right, at (+370, −10). To the left, at (−370, −10), a small G-dot. Rank 5: Titillium Web labels and the logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** The bloom. The opening follows the revs: d = R × (rpm − idle) ÷ (shift rpm − idle), held at R above the shift point. At idle every petal lies on the same disc and the flower is closed. As the revs climb the petals part, and the outline grows from Ø220 to Ø440. At the shift point d = R, every petal passes exactly through the middle, and the rim of each petal lights in the `shift` colour, steadily. On an upshift one more petal unfolds out of its neighbour while the revs fall and the flower half closes: the angles go from 360° ÷ N to 360° ÷ (N + 1) on the `snap` spring. Where petals overlap the glass is deeper, so the rosette shows how many petals cover each point: k petals pass T^k of the light under them. On the limiter only the beads strobe. Lateral G shears the two petal depths and the core by 0, 2 and 4 px per g.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max with the car's per-gear thresholds → the opening d, the arc, the beads and the limiter; gear → the numeral and the petal count (neutral is the closed disc; reverse is one petal that points down); speed → the tabular speed; throttle → the brightness of the light under the flower; acceleration → the G-dot on the `gBall` spring and the parallax. Every one of these is a verified field.
- **References:** The Mindfulness app of Apple Vision Pro and the Breathe animation of Apple Watch; rosettes of equal overlapping circles; `VisionSpatialGlass` and `LiquidGlass` in this catalog for the glass and the handover format; `docs/specs/optics.md` for Beer–Lambert, Snell and Fresnel
- **Phase 6 assets:** The petals are the kit's clear optical glass, each a shallow lens: Blender bakes one petal's normal and thickness maps at 4K from a modelled blank, and the same pair serves every petal. ComfyUI: the studio panorama the rims reflect. Penpot: the board with the flower at gears 1, 3, 6 and 10. No texture is painted inside the glass. MSDF atlases for Saira 300, Saira Condensed 700 and Titillium Web 600. The kit's analytic studio is the fallback.
- **Risk:** Blades round a centre are what `ApertureNine` has: these are full circles of clear glass, they overlap, and their count is the gear. In first and second gear the flower is one petal and then two: that is the cue working, not a fault, and the direction check looks at gears 1 and 2 first. Ten overlapping panes can go grey: the tint comes from absorption only, the summed depth is capped at six petals' worth, and the gear keeps its black core. Glass is the costly paint: if ten discs pass 0.3 ms, the far ring of petals is drawn as rims only. Perf: at most ten glass discs in one glass layer, one arc and ten beads, est. 0.3 ms.
