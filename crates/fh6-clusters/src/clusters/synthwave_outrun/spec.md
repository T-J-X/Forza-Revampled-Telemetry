# Synthwave Outrun (`SynthwaveOutrun`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 20 (SynthwaveOutrun — build #21 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 21

A night-drive horizon in letterbox: 1700x400 on the hood, 1400x520 in the cockpit, split by a 1 px horizon line at y -40. Rank 3: a sliced half-sun (radius 170) behind the gear, its 14 horizontal slats lighting bottom-up with rpm, gaps narrowing toward the crown, accent at the base to secondary at the top, at 70% alpha. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear (cap 210, 10 deg MSDF shear) in ink over a feathered dimming pad, under a 36 px 16-pod crown strip. Rank 2: chrome-banded italic speed (cap 104) to the right, with a hard horizon cut inside each glyph aligned to the scene horizon. Rank 4: a perspective hairline grid below, at 20% luminance or less, unglowing. Rank 5: a wireframe ridge on the horizon.

- **Signature moment:** Sunrise to the redline. Each slat is an rpm stage that lights in the same frame, so the sun rises with the revs and closes into a full disc at the shift rpm while the crown strip strobes at 10 Hz. The steady limiter cue is the horizon line heating to accent; it never flashes. Upshift: as the sun sinks with the rpm drop, a 1-frame bright pulse runs outward along the horizon from X=1920 at 4000 px/s. Boot: the grid draws in from the horizon in 4 sectors, then the sun rises to live rpm.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm with per-car thresholds drives the sun slats and the strip. Gear drives the hero glyph and Speed the chrome speed. DistanceTraveled sets the grid scroll phase, so a parked car's grid is still. AngularVelocityY / max(v, 3 m/s) sets the grid curvature kappa; this is a synthesised road plane, not map data. PositionY over the 120-point trail draws the horizon ridge as the car's own recent elevation profile, labelled as such. Felt lateral G sets the light azimuth that slides the chrome bands' highlight. Brake fades the grid's near edge toward caution as a steady ramp.
- **References:** Generic 1980s synthwave/outrun poster and album art (sliced sun, chrome type, wire grid); Tron (1982) and Tron: Legacy line work (GMUNK, Digital Domain); 1980s arcade racing cabinets' horizon framing; Oblivion GFX restraint
- **Phase 6 assets:** Saira Condensed 800 MSDF atlas (tnum check). The chrome band ramp as a 256x1 LUT baked at load. An optional 4K chrome-glyph bevel normal map (procedural bevel fallback). The sun, grid and ridge are procedural.
- **Risk:** The large lit sun threatens the colour budget and burn-in. At idle only one slat is lit (20% luminance or less), slats sit at 70% alpha with 4 px gaps, warning red is never a slat colour, and the sun never flashes. The chrome cut must not cost legibility: speed keeps a minimum band L so it passes the 4.5:1 blur gate, and the gear stays solid ink on a pad. The grid is drawn as analytic SDF lines in one pass clipped to the lower 160 px band (about 0.05 ms). Total is about 0.2 ms.
