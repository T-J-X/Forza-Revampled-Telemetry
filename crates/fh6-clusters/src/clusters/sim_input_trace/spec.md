**A sim racer's input overlay as a dash** (TJX, 2026-11-28: after the clean Gran Turismo variants, "some more sim like"). Sim racing has its own instrument that no road car carries: the input telemetry overlay that streamers and coaches run over iRacing, Assetto Corsa Competizione and rFactor 2, with scrolling traces of throttle, brake, clutch and steering beside the pedal bars. Step 1 researches the real ones (the common overlay tools, and the trace views of MoTeC i2 and the sims' own telemetry screens) and names the one this dash is benchmarked against.

Starting layout, on a strict grid with tabular figures. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 200) at (0, −110), under a row of 16 shift LEDs (780 × 32) in the green, red, blue order of sim hardware. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) to the right of the gear. Rank 3: a segmented rev bar under the LEDs, numbered from `EngineMaxRpm`. Rank 4: the trace window, 1000 × 170 at (0, +110): the last 8 s scroll from right to left at a fixed 125 px a second, with throttle, brake and steering each on the same 0 to 100% height, and at its right edge the live pedal bars and a steering tick. Rank 5: labels `THR`, `BRK`, `STR`, a time grid every second and the logo zone. Hood view: the trace window alone, 1500 × 110, with gear and speed at its right end.

- **Signature moment:** The shift mark. Every gear change drops a thin vertical line into the trace window with the new gear's number above it, so the driver can read the last eight seconds like a lap coach: where the brake came off, where the throttle came back in, and how far apart they were. Time spent on the limiter is hatched on the rev row above the traces, and time with neither pedal pressed is marked `COAST` on the baseline. On the limiter only the LED row strobes, as sim wheels do; nothing in the trace window flashes.
- **Telemetry:** throttle, brake and steer (all verified) → the three traces, sampled once a frame into fixed ring buffers of 512 values each, no allocation; rpm, idle and max with per-gear thresholds → LED row, rev bar and limiter hatching; gear → numeral and shift marks; speed → tabular speed; acceleration → an optional fourth trace, longitudinal G. Clutch and handbrake are decoded but not covered by the golden packets: they join the traces only after the real-game check (97% rule). No ABS or traction-control lamp is drawn: the packet has neither.
- **References:** Input telemetry overlays for iRacing and Assetto Corsa Competizione; MoTeC i2 time-distance graphs; Fanatec and Simucube wheel LED rows; `BoschDdu` in this catalog for the Race Dash grid
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the LED row as a rendered bar of lens caps in a machined carrier, and a slim anodised frame edge for the trace window with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: the anti-glare matrix of the display face and a dark anodised surface for the carrier. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800, Chakra Petch 500 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** Three traces told apart by colour alone fail for colour-blind drivers: each trace has its own weight and style (throttle a solid 3 px line, brake a filled area, steering a 1.5 px line about the centre) and its label at the right edge. Long polylines are expensive in the kit (one 128-point line cost 0.36 ms in the pilot): draw each trace as a height-field ribbon or in chunks of at most 8 segments, and measure before anything else is built. A scrolling window pulls the eye: it scrolls at a constant speed, sits at rank 4 luminance and never blooms. Est. 0.3 ms, the highest of the Motorsport dashes.
