# AR Nav Wayfinder (`ArNavWayfinder`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 17 (ArNavWayfinder — build #20 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** ADAS & HUD (Design Council 3D), spec [docs/design/families/adas-hud.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/adas-hud.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 20

A first-person 'through the glass' road inside the binnacle (eye 1.1 m, FOV 40°, no ego car), its vanishing point pinned on X=1920 at y −140 exactly where the hero gear stands (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170), so every line leads to the gear. Z1: ten 48×38 px wayfinder chevrons at y −292, five per side pointing inward, filling outside-in. Rank 3: the two road edges are the tach, 5 px light rails filling from the bottom corners toward the gear, parametrised by screen length. Speed sits on the horizon at left (Saira Light, cap 112), lap delta and next-gate distance at right (Titillium). Z4 between the rails: the remembered road as a 1.6 m driving-line ribbon with 1 px brake-gate arches and apex diamonds; uncharted road shows only a fading κ arc.

- **Signature moment:** The target-gear ghost. 250 m before a remembered brake gate, the gear you used there on your best pass appears as a ghost numeral (inkGhost, cap 90) just beyond the hero gear at the vanishing point, with one tick per downshift still needed. Each downshift steps the hero toward it, and when they match the two merge with a 1-frame 60% rim flash. Passing a gate drops its arch flat onto the ribbon, and the next arch rises from the vanishing point over 200 ms. The limiter strobes only the chevron strip, while the rails hold a steady full shift-colour fill.
- **Telemetry:** Road-ahead knowledge is self-mapped from our own history, never from the game: a preallocated 65,536-sample memory (x, y, z, speed, brake, throttle, gear every 2 m, ≈1 MB, spatially hashed in 32 m cells) of roads already driven this session plus the best lap. World position + yaw → memory writes and nearest-sample localisation (±64-sample window, heading match ±30°); local velocity + angular velocity y → κ arc when uncharted; speed → ribbon scroll and gate distance; recorded brake onset → gate placement; recorded throttle/brake → ribbon tint (accent / caution); recorded gear vs live gear → target-gear ghost and downshift ticks; rpm → chevrons and rails; lap number, current/best lap time and distance travelled → best-lap reference swap and delta (Saira Condensed tabular, go/warning by sign); roll → horizon counter-tilt.
- **References:** Mercedes MBUX AR navigation arrows (judged slow, so ours are never delayed); highway-in-the-sky tunnel-gate avionics displays; road chevron-alignment signs; circuit brake-marker boards at 100/50 m
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (SDF rails, chevrons, gate arches, apex diamonds, a ribbon triangle strip); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600; the shared blue-noise tile
- **Risk:** The risk is memory and localisation, not pixels. Preallocate the 1 MB ring and spatial hash at start (no per-frame allocation), bound the nearest-sample search, and require a heading match so a crossing road is never taken for the one ahead. Gates are dashed after one pass and turn solid only when a second pass agrees within 4 m, so a mis-registered gate never reads as truth. Lap number, lap times and distance travelled are not decoded yet: add them against tests/golden/packets (97% rule) or integrate speed·dt. GPU <0.08 ms.
