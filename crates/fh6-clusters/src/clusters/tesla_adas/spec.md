# Tesla ADAS (`TeslaAdas`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 15 (★ TeslaAdas — build #5 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** ADAS & HUD (Design Council 3D), spec [docs/design/families/adas-hud.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/adas-hud.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 5

Clay-and-light diorama floating frameless in the column gap. Z1: ten 60×22 px pill pods at y −296, filling outside-in onto the centre pair, over a 4 px power ribbon (rank 3, ±640 px), primary→accent with a 12 px white comet. Band y −230..−80: speed left (Saira Light, cap 112, ghost 8s), gear centre (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170) in a 1 px vertical gear pill with n−1/n+1 as Titillium ghosts, rpm and shy SLIP/LOCK/LIM chips right. Z4: chase-cam road (eye 0/1.85/−3.4 m, FOV 35°), ground ≤6% luminance, 120 px dissolve masks; glTF clay cars lit half-Lambert by the G-driven light with 1 px silhouette rims, number-free FSD lock brackets, 3-step blind-spot arcs; the ego is a brand-pearl digital twin.

- **Signature moment:** Brake dive. In the frame brake passes 5%, the ego twin's lamps light in warning, its nose dips by the measured pitch (×3, clamped 4°, fast spring), and a 2 px caution stop line drops across the lanes at d = v²/(2·max(−aLong, 2 m/s²)), labelled in Titillium cap 16 (hidden beyond 120 m); the path carpet beyond it falls to 35% alpha. Each upshift sends one 250 ms accent wake up the carpet (alpha only, no bloom) while the gear pill rolls 24 px. At the limiter only the pod strip strobes 10 Hz; the underglow holds a steady limiter violet and the gear may pulse 2 Hz after 0.5 s.
- **Telemetry:** rpm/max/idle → pods (per-car, per-gear thresholds) and power ribbon; gear → pill and neighbour ghosts; speed → readout, dash scroll at true ground speed, carpet length; brake + local accel z → brake lamps, stop line, carpet dimming; pitch/roll minus a 2 s baseline → ego dive and roll; normalised suspension travel ×4 → per-corner ride height; steer → front-wheel angle; wheel rotation speeds → tyre sidewall stripe; angular velocity y / max(v, 3 m/s) → road curvature κ; local velocity x/z → β ego yaw and slip sector; slip ratio ×4 → SLIP (driven wheels > 0.1 on throttle) / LOCK (fronts < −0.1 on brake) chips and caution underglow, since the packet has no assist flags; tyre temps ×4 → shy contact-patch thermal steps shown only outside the optimal window; car ordinal → brand → ego tint. Traffic is a seeded ambient simulation (the packet has no traffic): it carries no distance numbers, is tagged synthetic in the OSD, and is off in the Zen tier.
- **References:** Tesla FSD v14 visualisation (grey world, blue in-path, two-tone path); Rivian R1 Unreal digital-twin cluster reacting to vehicle state; Porsche Taycan 3D assistance view (3 lanes, 6 road users cap); Lucid Gravity floating OLED and Zen theme
- **Phase 6 assets:** glTF ego coupe (≤5k tris, separable wheels, brake-lamp and underbody material slots) plus 3 clay traffic meshes (sport sedan, SUV, supercar, ≤5k tris each); sprite fallbacks assets/adas_ego_coupe.png, adas_traffic_sedan.png, adas_traffic_suv.png; procedural box-car last-resort fallback; 64×64 blue-noise tile for the ground gradient; MSDF atlases Saira Light, Saira Condensed 800, Titillium Web 600
- **Risk:** The scene can outshout ranks 1–2, and mesh cost. Clip the mesh pass to the 1440×280 Z4 rectangle with 4× MSAA there only (≈0.04 ms), ≤7 draws, no emissive writes from Z4 (the underglow is a baked Gaussian quad, not bloom), scene luminance capped at 40% except the ego and one in-path object, and the speed must pass the blur gate over the ground. Exaggerated pitch/roll can feel seasick: baseline, clamp and spring them. The hero frame's '26 m' on a synthetic lead car is fake data and is dropped. Hood [4]: the scene widens to ±880 × 180 px at 25° pitch, pods at y −206. Total ≈0.15 ms.
