# Cymatic Resonance Field (`CymaticResonanceField`): brief

- **Family:** Fluid / Generative (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/)

An acoustic-levitation bench. A polished black-chrome circular plate seen at a 62° tilt (ellipse 1100×300, centre (0,+90)) carries 20k GPU sand grains that gather on the nodal lines of circular-plate modes J_m(k_mn·r)·cos mθ. Its near rim, 9 o'clock round to 3 o'clock, is the tach (rank 3): a 6 px lit ribbon with Barlow Semi Condensed numerals inside; the far rim is a 1 px hairline. The gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200) levitates above the plate centre at (0,−130) on a dimming pad; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 112) at (+330,−120). Fifteen 10×36 tuning-fork tines form the shift strip at y −296. Grains are rank 4: inkMuted tinted 18% primary, no bloom; the centre clamp carries the crest.

- **Signature moment:** An upshift re-tunes the plate. The grains are thrown off in one impulse (about 120 ms of airtime along the plate normal, foreshortened by the tilt) and land on the nodal lines of the new, lower mode, so the whole figure re-forms in about 300 ms. The gear glyph itself changes in frame 1. Modes step through 9 discrete (m, n) figures across the car's idle → shift range with 120 rpm hysteresis, never a continuous morph, so each rpm band has its own recognisable figure. Limiter: the plate holds the highest mode and the grains warm steadily to `shift`; only the tine strip strobes at 10 Hz.
- **Telemetry:** rpm → near-rim tach ribbon (`ribbon` spring with feed-forward, exact) and the mode index (9 bands from the car profile's idle → shift rpm). Per-gear thresholds → tines, outside-in. gear → glyph plus the re-tune impulse. speed → tabular. throttle → drive amplitude: on a lift the lines are soft and fuzzy, at full throttle they are razor-thin. steer + gLat → figure rotation θ0 on the `light` spring (±20°). gLong → grains creep along the tilt under braking. Surface rumble (decoder extension needed; verify the offset on tests/golden/packets) and suspension-travel velocity → grain-hop impulses that are zero on smooth tarmac, so idle is still. The Phase 8 continuous mapping n = rpm/1200, m = V/40 is replaced by stepped modes; the square-plate Chladni equation stays as the procedural fallback figure.
- **References:** Ernst Chladni's sand-figure plates (1787) and modern Chladni-plate demonstrations; Hans Jenny, Cymatics (1967); Bessel-mode visualisations of a vibrating circular membrane; Bang & Olufsen sculptural black-aluminium audio hardware for the plate finish.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only. Bessel J_0..J_6 root table and 512-sample radial profiles computed at load into an RGBA16F LUT; the plate rim bevel normal is analytic. Optional: a polished black-chrome rim normal map. 128² blue-noise for grain seeding. Exo 2, Barlow Semi Condensed and Titillium Web MSDF. Brand crest dial variant for the centre clamp.
- **Risk:** The v1 build was a white full-canvas wash with a square pattern and no gear; 20k grains could also read as noise behind the readouts. Keep the plate wholly in Z4 (only its far rim rises behind the gear), grains at alpha ≤0.5 and ≤20% luminance when settled, no bloom, and dimming pads so gear and speed pass the blur gate. The grains must never be read as the tach; the rim ribbon is the exact reading. Perf: grain gradient descent on −∇(u²) via LUT for 20k points ≈0.02 ms, instanced 2 px sprites ≈0.04 ms, rim and tach SDF ≈0.02 ms. Hood [4] fit (1760×400): plate 1500×220, gear at gearCompact cap 150, speed to its right.
