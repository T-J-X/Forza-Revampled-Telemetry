# Solaris Warp Core (`SolarisWarpCore`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 24 (SolarisWarpCore — build #26 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 26

A fusion containment rig. Two vertical magnetic-bottle columns, 380 px tall, 90 px wide and 520 px apart, flank the gear. Each is ringed by 10 D-shaped coil hoops drawn as 1 px titanium rims lit by the G light; the space between them stays black. Rank 3: plasma rises in both columns in step, a soft-edged filament with a primary-to-white core whose waist pinches with boost. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear (cap 220) between the columns, under a 36 px 12-pod strip spanning the column caps. Rank 2: Saira Condensed 700 speed (cap 96) under the gear. Rank 4: four unglowing tyre-thermal cells at the column bases in the 4-step thermal colours. Power and torque sit in Chakra Petch micro-labels.

- **Signature moment:** Arc bridge. At the shift rpm both plasma columns reach their caps and a steady plasma arc (6 px core, shift colour) bridges over the gear between them; the arc never flickers, and only the strip strobes at 10 Hz. Boost compresses each filament from 40 to 22 px, and lateral G pushes the plasma toward one wall by up to 18 px. Upshift: each coil the plasma drops through gives a 1-frame 60% rim flash, top to bottom, within 50 ms. Boot: the coils energise in pairs from the base in 5 steps.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm drives the plasma height and the strip (per-car thresholds). Boost drives the filament pinch and core brightness. Felt lateral G drives the plasma deflection and the light azimuth. Gear drives the glyph and Speed the speed. TireTemp FL/FR/RL/RR drive the thermal cells (cold / optimal / hot / overheated, 150 ms crossfade). Power and Torque drive the readouts under the right and left columns. Fuel sets a 1 px level tick on the left base. DistanceTraveled advects the plasma noise, so a parked car is still.
- **References:** Tokamak and stellarator coil-hall photography (JET, Wendelstein 7-X); Z-pinch plasma physics diagrams; Tron: Legacy three-term neon; Oblivion GFX restraint
- **Phase 6 assets:** An optional 16-frame plasma-filament flipbook with a flow map (procedural curl-noise fallback). A coil-hoop normal strip (procedural SDF torus-section fallback). Saira Condensed and Chakra Petch MSDF atlases.
- **Risk:** Two tall glowing columns could out-shout the gear, and clock-driven plasma would break 'idle is still'. Column plasma peaks at 70% luminance while the gear is 100% ink on a dimming pad, and the noise advects by distance and boost, never by the clock. Cost: two 90x380 noise columns plus bloom, about 0.12 ms; the arc is a single SDF capsule.
