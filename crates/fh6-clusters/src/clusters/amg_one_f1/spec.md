- **Category:** Hypercar · **Target:** `recreation` · **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit
- **Family:** Race Dash (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/race-dash.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/race-dash.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/race-dash/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/race-dash/)

**Real display first.** The Mercedes-AMG ONE (in the game as the 2021 Mercedes-AMG One) carries a Formula 1 power unit that revs to 11,000 rpm. What the sources say: two free-standing 10-inch displays, one in front of the driver and one on the centre console angled toward the driver; a Formula 1-style wheel with a flattened top and bottom and a row of shift lights across the top of the rim; drive programs Race Safe, Race, EV, Race Plus, Strat 2 and Individual; aero states Highway, Track and Race DRS. No text source documents the driver display's graphics. **Take from frame grabs in step 1:** the whole screen layout, the shift-light count, colours and firing order, the typeface and the colours.

Starting layout: the driver's screen as a floating 680 × 380 slab with small rounded corners in a thin machined housing (the real display has one, so this dash may), and above it a detached 780 px arc of the wheel rim in carbon that carries the shift lights, 34 px tall. Rank 1: the rim lights and the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 220) in the screen centre. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) under the gear. Rank 3: a rev band along the screen top, numbered from `EngineMaxRpm` up to 11. Rank 4: power on the right as a bar with a tabular value, pedal traces on the left, and a lap delta line under the speed. Rank 5: the drive-program label, shown only as the dash's own name plate, and the logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** Lights on the rim. The shift lights follow the real wheel's order and colours once the frames confirm them (the family rule is that shift lights behave like the real hardware, and it wins over the outside-in default). The rim arc sits apart from the screen, so the lights reach the eye before the screen does. At the shift rpm the whole row is lit; on the limiter only the row strobes, 10 Hz, 50% duty, and reduced-flash mode swaps the strobe for a 2 Hz breath. On an upshift the row clears in 2 frames and the gear enters at 1.12 → 1.0 with no other motion on screen.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → rim lights (per-car, per-gear thresholds), rev band and limiter; gear → centre numeral; speed → tabular speed; power → the power bar, in `signals.power_unit`; throttle and brake → pedal traces; boost → a shy readout; speed and timestamp → the acceleration timers shared with `AmgMbuxTrackPace`. The hybrid system's charge and deployment, the drive program and the aero state are not in the telemetry: none of them is drawn as a live value, and no DRS or battery state is invented.
- **References:** Mercedes-AMG ONE press material and interior photos; owner and press videos of the ONE's driver display for frame grabs; the Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula 1 steering-wheel displays; `BoschDdu` in this catalog for the Race Dash grid and lamp rules
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the screen housing and the carbon rim arc with its LED lens caps, with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: the rim's carbon weave and the anti-glare matrix of the screen face. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** With no documented graphics this is the AMG dash most likely to be invented: no layout work before the frame grabs are in `RESEARCH.md`, and where frames cannot be found the handover says which parts are this thread's own design. A Formula 1 row of green, red and blue lights uses colour as its cue: the position along the row and the fill order carry the same message. An 11,000 rpm scale must still read for a car that revs to 6,000: number the band from the car's own maximum. Perf: one housing plate, a row of instanced lights, est. 0.12 ms.
