The phantom wheel. This cockpit is wheel-less, so the cluster becomes the rim. A Ø540 carbon-and-titanium instrument ring rotates with steering input (±32° at full lock) while a gyro-held core inside it stays level, as on the Jesko. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 180, inside a fixed W-shaped reference symbol; 13 chevron pods sit on a level shelf (32 × 740 px) that never rotates. Rank 2: tabular B612 Mono Bold speed, cap 90, in a level window under the gear. Rank 3: a 9 px primary-colour rev arc with a white leading edge on the rotating ring, Barlow Semi Condensed numerals, and shift at the ring's 12 index. Rank 4: a horizon hairline and pitch ladder behind the core at ≤20% luminance. Rank 5: four level corner micro-labels, and the crest on the ring at 6.

- **Signature moment:** Counter-steer split. In a drift the ring turns with the counter-steer, the horizon tilts with body roll the other way, and the level core holds the gear dead still between them. Three layers move three ways and read as 'the car rotating, the hands catching it'. At the limiter only the level chevron shelf strobes. The ring's 12 o'clock index fills with a steady shift colour wherever the ring is turned. On first launch the ring self-tests −32° → +32° → 0 inside the 350/300 ms sweep.
- **Telemetry:** steer (i8, −127..127) drives the ring rotation on a critically damped 12 rad/s spring, clamped to ±32°. roll and pitch drive the horizon tilt and ladder offset (spring ω ≈10 rad/s); roll's sign and units must be verified on tests/golden/packets (97% rule). rpm drives the rev arc (ribbon spring with feed-forward) and the shelf thresholds. gear and speed drive the level core. Yaw rate (angular_velocity_y) and slip angle drive a small drift caret on the horizon. Lateral G drives the light azimuth over the carbon's anisotropic sheen and the parallax (core 0, horizon 2, ring 4 px). IsRaceOn 0→1 (first launch only) plays the self-test.
- **References:** Koenigsegg Jesko gyroscopic wheel display (key information kept upright as the rim turns); aircraft attitude indicator with a fixed W reference; F1 steering-wheel displays
- **Phase 6 assets:** Carbon twill (12 px) albedo with a normal map and anisotropic sheen for the ring band; brushed titanium for the inner rim; the brand logo dial variant; MSDF atlases for B612 Mono, Saira Condensed, Barlow Semi Condensed and Titillium Web. Procedural fallbacks: a 2×2 twill and conic banding.
- **Risk:** v1 drew a hatched grey plate with empty boxes. Here, a rotating rev scale loses some reading accuracy and can feel unsettling at full lock. Cap the rotation at ±32° on a non-overshooting spring and keep ranks 1–2 (gear, speed and the shift shelf) level. Offer a 'fixed ring' toggle in the OSD. Roll trust is unverified: if the golden packets show it unusable, derive the horizon tilt from lateral G with a clamp and label it as derived. Performance: SDF only, est. 0.15 ms.
