**Inspired by the Gordon Murray T.50** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "some hypercars and supercars inspiration clusters"; in the game as the 2022 Gordon Murray T.50). Recalled, and to confirm in step 1 from Gordon Murray Automotive's own material, because the repo holds no source for this car yet: the driver sits on the centre line and faces one analogue rev counter, about 120 mm across and lit by flood lamps instead of from behind, with a screen on each side; the Cosworth V12 revs to 12,100 rpm; the controls are rotary knobs, with no touchscreen. Not established: the dial's end numeral, its red zone, its colours and typeface, what each screen shows, and whether it has shift lights. The idea taken from the car: an instrument that makes no light of its own. Only the gear, the speed and the shift lights emit; everything else is seen by the lamps.

Starting layout, mirrored exactly about the centre line, as the car is. Rank 3: one rev counter, Ø480 at (0, −10), with a satin chapter ring that carries the numerals, a matt black centre and a long machined needle that floats above the face. Two small hooded flood lamps sit on the rim at 35° each side of 12 o'clock. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 150) on a Ø200 cap over the needle's hub, so the needle passes under it, and 10 lamps on a straight strip above the dial (760 × 28), filling outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 84) in the open sector at the foot of the scale, inside the dial. Rank 4: two equal panes, 300 × 220 at (±470, 0), power with a peak hold on the left and a G-ball on the right. Rank 5: engraved labels on the ring and a maker's plate.

- **Signature moment:** The shadows. Each lamp throws the needle's shadow on the chapter ring, so the needle always has two soft shadows, and they are computed, not painted: a point of the needle at height h above the face, lit by a lamp at height H, lands at L + (P − L)·H / (H − h), where L is the lamp's place and P the point's place on the face. The shadows are long and spread wide at low revs, shorten as the needle climbs toward the lamps, and fold under the needle as it passes between them. Over the last tenth of the revs before the shift point the lamps turn from white to the `shift` colour, steadily and without a flash, so the ring and the needle change colour by light alone. On the limiter only the strip above the dial strobes.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → needle (`needle` spring with feed-forward), the scale's end numeral (the next whole thousand above max rpm, so the T.50 reads 0 to 13), red zone, lamp colour, the strip and the limiter; gear → centre cap; speed → tabular speed; power → left pane, with the peak of the current pull held until the next pull begins; acceleration → G-ball on the `gBall` spring; throttle and brake → one thin bar on the inner edge of each pane. The car's GT and Power engine maps are not in the telemetry and are not shown.
- **References:** Gordon Murray T.50 interior and its central rev counter; the McLaren F1's central instrument binnacle, for the layout's origin; floodlit aircraft instruments with post lamps; sundials and gnomon geometry for the shadow; `PorscheAnalog` in this catalog for the rendered-dial method
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the dial as real parts (chapter ring in satin aluminium with engraved, paint-filled numerals; matt centre; the needle and the centre cap machined from solid; the two hooded lamps; a thin polished bezel), rendered once per lamp so the app can mix each lamp's brightness and colour. ComfyUI: the fine circular brushing of the chapter ring, a leather-wrapped binnacle surround for the edges and reflections, and the weave of the panes' carbon frames. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800 and Barlow Semi Condensed 500.
- **Risk:** An instrument lit only by lamps can look dead on an OLED: the gear, the speed and the strip are emissive and carry ranks 1 and 2, and the numerals on the ring must still pass the contrast gate with the lamps at their dimmest. A shadow can be read as a second needle: both shadows stay soft-edged, are never darker than 40% of the ring's lit level, and are drawn only on the ring. One round dial with numerals is close to `PorscheAnalog` and `HakosukaS20`: this dash has one dial and no tubes, visible lamps, exact mirror symmetry and a face that is black where no light falls. The car has no brand palette in the game (it uses the standard palette), so the dash takes its colour from the tokens and never from an invented GMA colour. Perf: two light-variant plates, one needle sprite, two shadow shapes of at most 8 segments each, est. 0.2 ms.
