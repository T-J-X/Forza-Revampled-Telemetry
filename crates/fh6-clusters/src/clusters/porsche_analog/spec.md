# Porsche Analog (`PorscheAnalog`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 7 (★ PorscheAnalog — build #3 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Classic Analog (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/classic-analog.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/classic-analog.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/classic-analog/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/classic-analog/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 3

Pattern-setter (★). A Ø520 centre tach under domed sapphire owns the binnacle. Two Ø330 satellites, speed on the left and a G/lap chrono on the right, tuck behind its titanium ring at −4 px parallax, which puts the speed 400 px from the gear. The faces are oledBlack; their sunburst finish shows only as a G-driven anisotropic lobe at ≤20% luminance. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 200, in a hollow hub that the needle orbits, under 15 lume jewels on a flat 38 × 760 px visor. Rank 2: tabular Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 112, with ghost 8s. Rank 3: a primary-colour dauphine needle over Barlow Semi Condensed numerals. A two-rate Track scale pins shift at 12, magnifies shift→limiter 4× into 12→1:30 and carries the lume operating band. Rank 4: G-ball and lap chrono. Rank 5: brushed titanium, with the crest or Michroma name in the dead quadrant.

- **Signature moment:** Plumb line. The jewels fill outside-in on per-car, per-gear thresholds with 0 ms attack. The last one is the centre jewel on X = 1920, and it lights in the same frame that the needle stands vertical beneath it. That jewel is a second light for the needle only, so its colour runs down the dauphine's polished ridge while the two facets keep their G-lit and shadow split. At the limiter only the 38 px visor strobes (10 Hz, 50% duty). The hollow-hub bearing ring holds a steady shift-colour glow, the one steady cue; it replaces the hero frame's full-face red wash. The gear may pulse at 2 Hz after 0.5 s on the limiter. Gear change: the token motion (old glyph to 0.92 in 50 ms, new glyph from 1.12 with a 24 px slide on the fast spring) plus one 60% rim flash that runs once round the hub ring.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the needle (needle spring T 0.07, ζ 0.75, velocity feed-forward) and the jewel thresholds. EngineMaxRpm and EngineIdleRpm, plus the car-profile shift, peak-torque and peak-power rpm, set the Track scale and the lume band. Fallbacks: shift = limiter × 0.97, and the band is learned from a fixed 64-bin rpm→power/torque max table filled from the packet's power and torque fields. gear drives the hub glyph. speed drives the tabular digits and the speed satellite's needle. Lateral G drives the light azimuth (light spring) and the 0/2/4 px parallax. G lat/long drive the G-ball; current, last and best lap drive the chrono sub-hands. IsRaceOn 0→1 (first launch only) plays the sweep. Torque and the lap fields are not yet decoded by fh6-core::telemetry; add them and verify them against tests/golden/packets.
- **References:** Porsche 992.2 Classic cluster with the rev counter rotated to put the shift point at 12; Porsche 550 Spyder tachometer (green operating band); Patek Philippe sunburst dial finishing; Bosch DDU / AiM LED-lens shift pods
- **Phase 6 assets:** Brushed-titanium albedo/roughness with a spun (tangential) anisotropy map (R strength, G rotation) for the rings; a sunburst anisotropy map for the faces; a sapphire-dome normal map; a polished-steel hub matcap; the brand-logo dial variant (mono white); MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed, Barlow Semi Condensed, Titillium Web and Michroma. Every item has a procedural fallback (conic banding, analytic sunburst, analytic dome).
- **Risk:** The hero frame fails three rules. Its jewel arc (≈160 × 530 px) breaks both the ≤42 px strobe strip and the ≥720 px span. Its speed sits 506 px from the gear (Z2 allows ≤420). Its redline floods the whole face red. The flat visor, the tucked satellites and the hub-ring cue fix all three. The remaining risk is that the two-rate scale misreads at mid-range. Mitigation: it is used only when the car profile has a shift rpm, with numerals every 1000 and 100 rpm ticks only inside the magnified wedge; otherwise the symmetric 270° token face is used. Performance: three analytic faces (~0.55 Mpx), ~180 instanced ticks, ~40 MSDF glyphs and a bloom chain clipped to the 1440 × 560 envelope, est. 0.2–0.3 ms.
