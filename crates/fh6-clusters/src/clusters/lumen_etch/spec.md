# Lumen Etch (`LumenEtch`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 30 (LumenEtch — build #43 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 43

A frameless, untinted 1200×500 crystal slab, seen only through two 1 px polished face edges offset 6 px to show its thickness. Every graphic is laser-etched frost lit by a virtual LED bar in the bottom edge. Etch brightness therefore falls with height, I = exp(−h/λ), with a 70% floor inside the gear and speed. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 210) as solid frosted etch with a crisp 1 px cut line, plus a 40 px strip of 17 etched dots fed by a top-edge LED bar. Rank 2: speed on the left, Saira 300 cap 112, ghost 8s. Rank 3: 40 radial etched lines on a 200° arch, one LED each. Rank 4: an etched tyre plan under the gear. Titillium Web labels.

- **Signature moment:** Light pours in. On show, the LED bars ramp the light reach λ from 0 to 600 px over 200 ms, so the cluster fills from the bottom up instead of fading in. On an upshift the new gear lights at 70% in the same frame and fills bottom-up to 100% in 50 ms. Its surplus light escapes sideways along two etched hairlines and runs up the slab's polished side edges as a 1 px glint at 4000 px/s. At the limiter only the top dot strip strobes at 10 Hz; the polished face edges hold a steady, non-emissive exit line in the shift colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm + per-gear thresholds → which of the 40 edge LEDs feed the etched arch and the 17-dot strip (outside-in). Gear and speed → etched glyphs. Throttle → light reach λ 480→640 px, so the slab glows deeper under load; this is bounded and never goes below the 70% floor on ranks 1–2. Acceleration x/z → face-edge thickness parallax and the light azimuth on the polished edges. Tyre temps (4 thermal steps) and per-wheel slip ratio/angle → tyre-plan etch colour and amber/red slip outlines. Brake → a caution tick on the tyre plan's front axle. Native fields only; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Edge-lit laser-engraved acrylic signage and awards; Oblivion (2013) Bubbleship UI as a hologram embedded in a glass cockpit (GMUNK); Lucid Gravity's bezel-free floating OLED; Tron: Legacy's layered relief light.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only. Etch masks are SDF/MSDF (Saira Condensed, Saira and Titillium Web atlases), and the frost uses the shared 128² blue-noise tile at ≤4% amplitude. No plate or bezel textures.
- **Risk:** Design risk: the exponential falloff dims the top of the gear below the blur gate, and dense etched lines moiré on the WRGB subpixels. To avoid it: a 70% floor inside the rank 1–2 masks, etched lines ≥3 px with SDF AA, frost amplitude ≤4%, and nothing lit outside the etch, so off pixels stay black. Performance: one SDF pass over the 1200×500 clip, plus bloom on the gear, arch and strip only. Estimate ≈0.15 ms.
