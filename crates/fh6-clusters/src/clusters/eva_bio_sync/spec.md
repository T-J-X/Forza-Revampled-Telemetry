# EVA Bio-Sync (`EvaBioSync`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 21 (EvaBioSync — build #22 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 22

A pilot-to-machine synchronisation monitor grown on the family's 24 px lattice. Two honeycomb wings, three cells deep (44 px cells, 4 px gaps, 1 px rims), sweep up from a 300 px pointy-top hexagonal gear capsule like a bioreactor membrane. Rank 3: cells ignite from the wingtips inward along one fixed spine path, mirroring the shift row, filled primary with a 2-stop nucleus gradient; unlit cells are neutral rims at alpha 22. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear (cap 210) in the capsule, with a 36 px 14-pod strip capping the wings. Rank 2: Saira Condensed 700 speed (cap 100) under the capsule. Rank 4: a hex-framed dual-trace sync scope under the speed, unglowing. The capsule rim is lit by the G light.

- **Signature moment:** Phase-lock. The scope draws the driver's demand (Steer) and the car's response (yaw rate) as two traces. With grip they fuse into one bright line and SYNC reads 100%. In a slide they split, and the gap between them shades caution, so oversteer and understeer read as a visible gap. Under braking below -0.2 g, 1 px octagonal barrier rims expand from the capsule in caution at 3 Hz or less and alpha 40 or less, settling at zero decel. Upshift: one hex ring ripples outward through the lattice in 150 ms. Limiter: the strip strobes at 10 Hz and the capsule rim holds steady in accent.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm drives the wing cells and the strip. Gear drives the capsule glyph and Speed the speed. Steer is compared with AngularVelocityY scaled by speed for the two traces; this is a bicycle-model proxy with a nominal 2.7 m wheelbase and 14:1 ratio, labelled as an estimate. TireCombinedSlip x4 gives SYNC % = 100 x (1 - mean combined slip, clamped). AccelerationZ (longitudinal G) drives the braking barrier rims. Felt lateral G sets the light azimuth. Accel and Brake drive two shy input ticks on the capsule base.
- **References:** Bioreactor and cell-culture monitoring displays; aerospace honeycomb sandwich panels; Oblivion GFX single-lattice discipline (GMUNK); The Expanse's faction-colour restraint (Pushing Pixels interview)
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: an instanced hex SDF lattice, with the traces as polyline SDFs. An optional 512x512 cell-nucleus noise for the lit-cell gradient (gradient fallback). Saira Condensed and Chakra Petch MSDF atlases.
- **Risk:** The old brief echoed a well-known anime UI. This direction drops every borrowed name, glyph and styling; the lattice, scope and capsule are original. Some 84 cells read slower than a bar, so the fill follows one fixed mirrored spine and rank 1 is carried by the strip. Cost: about 90 hex SDFs in one instanced draw plus two 256-point polylines, about 0.2 ms with bloom on the lit cells only.
