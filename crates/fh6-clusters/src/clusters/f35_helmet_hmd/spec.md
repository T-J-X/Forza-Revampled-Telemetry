# F-35 Helmet HMD (`F35HelmetHmd`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 18 (F35HelmetHmd — build #19 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** ADAS & HUD (Design Council 3D), spec [docs/design/families/adas-hud.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/adas-hud.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 19

Collimated helmet symbology in one hue, the text-safe palette primary (a red brand primary hands over to accent so red is never structural; 'phosphor' #3CFF7A is a custom preset). Strokes are 2 px with 1 px SDF AA on black, and nothing is filled except glyph cores. Z1: a 760 px lock line of twelve 52×6 px dashes at y −300, filling outside-in. Gear top-centre (Saira Condensed 700, cap 170) inside four 1 px corner brackets. Left: a scrolling speed tape with a notched readout box (B612 Mono, cap 96) at x −380; right: an rpm tape with 250 rpm ticks, hatched redline and shift caret. Z4 below: a frameless 560×240 attitude window with faded edges, a roll-rotated pitch ladder (×4) with solid positive and dashed negative rungs, a fixed boresight and a winged flight-path marker. A 6 px phosphor halo on ranks 1–3 only.

- **Signature moment:** Lock and SHIFT cue. As the lock line fills outside-in, at the shift rpm the four corner brackets snap into a closed box round the gear and a boxed 'SHIFT' cue (B612 Mono, shift colour) lights steady above it. At the limiter only the lock line and the box's 4 px top bar strobe 10 Hz. In a drift the flight-path marker slides off the boresight by β (14 px/°, clamped ±35°), a 1 px leash joins them, and β in degrees hangs off the marker's wing. Below 5 m/s the marker fades out over 120 ms.
- **Telemetry:** rpm → lock-line dashes (per-car, per-gear thresholds), rpm tape, SHIFT cue, top-bar strobe; gear → hero; speed → speed tape and box; roll → ladder rotation (×2, spring); pitch minus a 2 s baseline → ladder offset (×4); local-frame velocity → FPM, with β = atan2(v_x, v_z) laterally and atan2(v_y, v_z) ×4 vertically (axes and signs verified against golden packets); local accel x/z → G readout and session max-G latch (B612 Mono, cap 20, rank 5); current lap time → bottom-right clock; yaw → an optional shy 3-digit heading box.
- **References:** F/A-18 and F-16 HUD conventions (fixed tapes, flight-path marker against boresight, asymmetric pitch ladder); NASA NTRS flight-path-marker HUD studies; the Airbus B612 cockpit typeface; Oblivion's hologram-in-glass line discipline
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (SDF strokes, tapes, ladder, brackets); MSDF atlases B612 Mono and Saira Condensed 700
- **Risk:** Symbology density can eclipse the rank order, and a car's attitude is too small to read. Hold the ladder at alpha 140 with only ±2 rungs drawn, no halo below rank 3, and exaggerated, sprung roll and pitch with the baseline removed. A single mid-green hue must still give the gear 7:1, so lift it to OKLCH L ≥ 0.80. v1 drew a filled green box and a full-canvas white wash, both banned. Draw only generic HUD conventions, never an exact copy of a real aircraft's format. Hood [4]: tapes move to ±560 and the attitude window shrinks to 760×150. Cost ≈300 SDF segments, <0.06 ms.
