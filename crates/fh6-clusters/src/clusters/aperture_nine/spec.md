# Aperture Nine (`ApertureNine`): brief

- **Category:** Liquid Glass · **Target:** `concept` · **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit
- **Family:** Modern Digital 2027 (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/modern-digital.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/modern-digital.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/)

**Category note.** In the Liquid Glass category the iris is seen through glass: lens elements of the kit's clear optical glass sit in front of the blades and refract them, and the blades stay black metal. Its family stays Modern Digital 2027. The iris geometry is in [Real geometry and maths](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md#real-geometry-and-maths).

A nine-blade camera iris at X 1920 is the tach. Its black-anodised titanium SDF blades are filled oledBlack and read only through 1 px machined edges and a brushed anisotropic lobe (≤18% luminance) that follow the G-driven light. Rpm rotates the blades: the aperture closes from r 200 at idle to r 128 at the car's shift rpm, and blade 1's tip points to the current rpm on a fixed 300° ring numbered in Barlow Semi Condensed 600 (cap 34). Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 200, always clear inside the aperture, under 15 outside-in pills (24 px) crowning the ring. Rank 2: Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 112, at +420. Rank 4: a camera light-meter ladder for longitudinal G directly beneath the speed.

- **Signature moment:** Shutter lock: at the car's shift rpm the nine blade edges close into one perfect circle around the gear numeral. Their rims step to a steady `shift` while only the pill crown strobes at 10 Hz. On an upshift the iris snaps open to the new rpm on the `ribbon` spring, with a 1-frame 60% edge flash, like a leaf shutter firing. In corners the highlight travels blade to blade around the ring.
- **Telemetry:** rpm/idle/max + per-car shift → blade rotation (linear in rpm), aperture radius, ring index, stage pills; rpm above shift → blade rim colour; gear → aperture glyph; speed → tabular speed; acceleration z (longitudinal G) → light-meter ladder (right = acceleration in go, left = braking in warning, 0 at centre); boost → shy '+0.8 BAR' readout styled as exposure compensation; lateral G → light azimuth (highlight runs edge to edge) and parallax (gear 0, blades −2, ring −4 px).
- **References:** Leica Summilux 9-blade iris diaphragms; Hasselblad leaf shutters; Gossen Lunasix exposure meter; Porsche 992.2 shift-at-12 track mode
- **Phase 6 assets:** A brushed anisotropic tangent map for the blades (strength in R, rotation in G) with an analytic fallback; shared MSDF Saira Condensed 600/800, Barlow Semi Condensed 600 and Titillium Web 600; everything else procedural.
- **Risk:** Design: a large rotating structure in the periphery could compete with the gear, and closing blades could clip the numeral. Fix: the blades are black with only their 1 px rims lit; the aperture is clamped at r ≥128 (the gear's half-diagonal is ≈119 px at cap 200); blade motion is direct telemetry on the ribbon spring with no overshoot, and idle is still. Perf: nine analytic blade SDFs in one 640×640 quad plus the ring ≈0.07 ms.
