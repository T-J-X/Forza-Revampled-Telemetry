# Aero Smoke Tunnel (`AeroSmokeTunnel`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 35 (AeroSmokeTunnel — build #39 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Modern Digital 2027 (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/modern-digital.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/modern-digital.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 39

A calm top-down wind tunnel sits under the gear and speed. A brand-tinted 1 px plan silhouette (sedan, SUV or supercar by class, 180 px, heading up) sits in a 900×200 flow window with mask-dissolved edges. Smoke streamlines wrap it, drawn as contour lines of an analytic stream function: Joukowski potential flow round an ellipse, rotated by slip β, plus a yaw-rate vortex. They are 2 px, ink at ≤22% luminance, with dashes that drift with distance travelled. Above: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 200, at X 1920; Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 112, at +320; a thin 1100 px crown ribbon tach (7→14 px) under modern11 diamonds. Titillium labels and a shy FLOW · MODEL tag.

- **Signature moment:** Crossflow: in a drift, the incoming smoke swings to the car's true direction of travel while the silhouette keeps its heading, so you see the air hit the flank at angle β. The wake sheds a counter-rotating vortex pair whose strength follows yaw rate. Heavy braking pinches the streamlines into a pale amber stagnation fan at the nose (steady, ≤30%). At standstill the dashes stop; at the limiter only the diamond strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** local velocity x/z → β and free-stream direction (hidden below 5 m/s); distance travelled → dash phase (still at rest); speed → streamline spacing; angular velocity y → vortex circulation Γ; brake + longitudinal G → stagnation pinch; car class/ordinal → silhouette choice; rpm/idle/max + shift → ribbon and diamonds; gear; speed; lateral G → light azimuth and parallax. The flow is an idealised model built from velocity and yaw, not aero data or CFD, and is labelled that way.
- **References:** F1 wind-tunnel smoke-rake visualisation; Joukowski potential-flow plots from classic aerodynamics texts; Tesla FSD ego state shown on the ego model; Lucid Gravity Zen-theme restraint
- **Phase 6 assets:** Three plan-view car silhouettes as SVG (sedan, SUV, supercar), rasterised once through resvg, with a procedural capsule fallback; shared MSDF Saira Condensed 600/800 and Titillium Web 600.
- **Risk:** Design: constantly flowing dashes in peripheral vision can pull attention from ranks 1–3 and be mistaken for real aero data. Fix: ≤22% luminance and no emissive; contours stay still at constant β (only the dashes move, and they stop at rest); hidden in the race and zen density tiers; an explicit MODEL tag. Perf: one analytic fragment pass (a complex sqrt and log per pixel) over 900×200 ≈0.03 ms, with no particles and no simulation.
