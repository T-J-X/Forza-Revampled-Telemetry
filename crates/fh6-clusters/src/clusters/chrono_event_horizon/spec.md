# Chrono Event Horizon (`ChronoEventHorizon`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 22 (ChronoEventHorizon — build #27 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 27

A miniature black hole in the binnacle. The gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 200, ink) floats in a 230 px shadow that is a feathered dimming pad (alpha up to 140 x (1 - scrim)), not paint. Rank 3: an accretion disk inclined 78 deg (an 1100x190 ellipse) whose lit arc grows clockwise from 7 o'clock with rpm. Its far side is lensed up over the shadow as a thin second arc, and a 1 px photon ring hugs the shadow. Doppler beaming changes intensity, never hue: the approaching side is primary lifted to white, the receding side 60%. Rank 1: a gently curved 34 px 15-pod strip along the lensed arc. Rank 2: Saira 200 tabular speed (cap 100) to the right. Rank 4: an embedding-funnel G meter under the speed.

- **Signature moment:** Mass is rpm. The shadow radius and lensing strength grow with rpm (Einstein radius 0.8 to 1.15x), so the gear visibly pulls the tach light around itself. At the shift rpm the lensed arc and photon ring close into a full Einstein ring, steady, in shift colour, while only the curved strip strobes at 10 Hz. Upshift: the disk's shear texture steps back one sector on the fast spring as the rpm falls. The disk advects with DistanceTraveled, so a parked car's disk is frozen. Boot: the disk assembles in 6 angular sectors around a shadow that is already live.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm drives the disk arc, shadow radius, lensing and strip. Gear drives the glyph and Speed the speed. The sign of AngularVelocityY picks the approaching side (Doppler intensity only). DistanceTraveled drives the disk-texture advection. Felt G (-AccelerationX/Z) places a test particle on the embedding funnel with a 500 ms trail and sets the light azimuth. Rear TireSlipAngle tilts the funnel by beta (event only). Boost thickens the photon ring from 1 to 2 px.
- **References:** DNEG's lensed accretion-disk renders and their 2015 Classical and Quantum Gravity paper on spinning black holes; the Event Horizon Telescope image of M87* (2019); Flamm's paraboloid embedding diagrams; Tron: Legacy's three-term light
- **Phase 6 assets:** A 512x512 tileable disk turbulence (FBM baked at load is the procedural fallback; optionally a Blender render with a matching flow map). Saira Light and Saira Condensed MSDF atlases. The shadow, lensing and funnel are analytic.
- **Risk:** True black is transparent on this overlay, so the shadow reads only through the scrim or the dimming pad. At 0% scrim the pad must keep the gear at 7:1 or better (blur gate). Lensing must not ray-march: it uses the analytic two-image approximation (primary ellipse plus a warped secondary arc) in one 1200x520 pass, about 0.15 ms. There are no red or blue Doppler hues, so red stays reserved.
