# Retro Cyber (`RetroCyber`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 19 (★ RetroCyber — build #6 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

> **2026-11-28 rebuild:** the dash now recreates the Toyota Supra A70 Panoramic Digital Meter. Where this brief and [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md) differ (the octagon tube, the 15-pod strip and the chevrons are gone), RESEARCH.md and its list of deliberate changes win. The brief below is kept as the catalog wrote it.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 6

A vacuum-fluorescent tube in a machined housing: a 10 px gunmetal chamfer45 ring with light-driven 28 px chamfers around a 1380x500 #000000 cavity. Four VFD layers: Seg7 phosphor (0 px); a 6 px hex grid that only modulates lit phosphor; five 1 px filament wires seen as dark lines across lit segments (+1 px); front glass shown only by a light-driven crescent and 1 px rim, never a tint (+3 px). Rank 1: octagon-bay Seg7 gear, cap 220, 45% white core, under a 38 px slanted 15-pod strip. Rank 2: Seg7 speed, cap 112, ghost 8s. Rank 3: a 24-chevron rising arc, 14 primary, 6 secondary, 4 accent. Rank 4: unglowing THR/BRK/BST columns and a 13x5 dot G-scope. Scanlines multiply emission.

- **Signature moment:** Filament ignition boot (the family's sector boot). On the first IsRaceOn 0->1 after launch, the five filaments warm as 1 px lines at 15% luminance for 150 ms. The cavity then ignites in 8 left-to-right sectors of 40 ms as an all-segments self-test that settles to ghost. Total time is 1 s or less, and gear and speed are live from frame 1. Gear change: a 1-frame 60% rim flash laps the octagon's 8 facets in 50 ms. Limiter: only the 38 px strip strobes at 10 Hz in shift, the octagon rim holds steady in accent, and the gear may pulse at 2 Hz after 0.5 s. Segments light in the same frame and release with a 50 ms phosphor decay, so the display never trails telemetry.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm against per-car, per-gear thresholds (EngineMaxRpm fallback) drives the strip and the 24 chevrons. Gear drives the octagon Seg7 (N in ink at 72%, R in warning). Speed drives the Seg7 speed with 0.6 hysteresis. Accel, Brake and Boost drive the THR (go), BRK (warning) and BST (secondary) columns; this fixes the v2 hero's pink brake column. Felt G (-AccelerationX/Z) drives the G-scope dot with a 500 ms trail, the light azimuth for chamfers and crescent, and 0/1/3/4 px parallax. Rear TireSlipAngle drives the SLIP lamp and the DRIFT n deg readout. TireSlipRatio together with Brake or Accel drives derived LOCK and SPIN lamps; these are inferred, because the packet has no ABS or TC flags, and are labelled as such. IsRaceOn drives the boot and the 1-frame hide.
- **References:** Noritake Itron and Futaba multi-segment VFD modules (filament, grid-mesh and phosphor stack); the 1976 Aston Martin Lagonda and 1980s Japanese digital dashboards; Cyberpunk 2077's squared 1980s car numerals (HUDS+GUIS); Oblivion GFX (GMUNK) line and greeble discipline
- **Phase 6 assets:** Chakra Petch MSDF atlas with a recorded tnum check. A 6 px hex-mesh tile with mips (procedural SDF fallback). A chamfer45 bezel normal/roughness map that supersedes assets/cyber_vfd_bezel.png (procedural chamfer fallback). A 64x64 blue-noise tile to dither the glass crescent. Seg7 digits are procedural.
- **Risk:** The mesh, filaments and scanlines could light off pixels or moire on the WRGB subpixels. All three only multiply lit phosphor, are fixed in screen space, and the mesh is mip-mapped, so the cavity stays #000000. Bloom for 24 chevrons plus 15 pods uses one emissive buffer clipped to 1440x560 with the 8-level chain from 256 px (about 0.12 ms). Everything else is SDF in one pass, about 0.3 ms total.
