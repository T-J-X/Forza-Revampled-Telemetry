**Inspired by the Lamborghini Revuelto** (in the game as the 2024 Lamborghini Revuelto). Recalled from Lamborghini's press material and to confirm in step 1, since no page could be opened when this brief was written: a 12.3-inch driver display beside an 8.4-inch centre screen and a 9.1-inch passenger display; drive modes Città, Strada, Sport and Corsa on a red rotary selector; a V12 that revs to 9,500 rpm; the Y and the hexagon as the two shapes that run through the whole car; and a Corsa look with a bar-like rev display and a large gear number. This dash takes the Corsa look, the Y and the hexagon and goes its own way with them. The name is checked against Lamborghini's own wording in step 1.

Starting layout, detached parts on true black. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 200) in a 300 px forged-carbon hexagon at (0, −30), and a shift strip drawn as the two upper arms of a Y: 2 × 6 slanted cells that run from the outer tips at (±390, −300) down to the hexagon's crown, filling outside-in, 34 px tall. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) directly under the hexagon. Rank 3: the rev track, a hexagonal ring around the gear whose six straight sides light in turn, numbered from `EngineMaxRpm` so the scale reaches 10 for the V12. Rank 4: two slanted wing panels that follow the Y's angle, power on the right and pedals with G on the left. Rank 5: the mode name plate and the bull.

- **Signature moment:** Y ignition. As the revs build the two arms of the Y burn inward from their tips. At the shift rpm they meet at the crown and the light drops down the stem into the hexagon, whose machined rim takes a steady `shift` colour: the Y is complete only at the moment to shift. On the limiter only the Y's arms strobe. On an upshift the stem empties first, then both arms fall back outward on the `ribbon` spring, and the six sides of the rev track relight to the new rpm.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max with the car's per-gear thresholds → Y arms, rev track, stem and limiter; gear → hexagon numeral; speed → tabular speed; power → right wing bar and value, in `signals.power_unit`; throttle and brake → left wing bars; acceleration → G dot on the `gBall` spring; lateral G → the light on the hexagon's chamfers. The car's drive mode and hybrid mode are not in the telemetry: the look is the driver's choice in the OSD (`config.extra` key `RevueltoCorsaLook`: Corsa by default, Strada as a calmer round variant), and nothing switches by itself.
- **References:** Lamborghini Revuelto driver display in Corsa and Strada, from Lamborghini's own press images and owner videos; the Revuelto's Y-shaped light signature and hexagonal details; Lamborghini Forged Composites; `ValkyrieLmp1` in this catalog for forged carbon, so the two do not look alike
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the hexagon pod (forged-carbon body, machined and anodised chamfers) and the slanted cell caps of the Y, with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: a forged-composite surface with larger, flatter chips than `ValkyrieLmp1` uses, and a hexagonal mesh for the wing panels. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** A hexagonal rev track is harder to read than an arc: number every side, keep a lit marker at the exact rpm, and check it against the blur gate. Slanted cells can alias on the OLED: analytic anti-aliasing and a minimum cell width of 30 px. The dash must not become a logo collage: the Y and the hexagon each appear once at full size. Lamborghini's own cluster colours are not established, so the palette roles lead until the frames are in. Perf: instanced cells and one pod plate, est. 0.15 ms.
