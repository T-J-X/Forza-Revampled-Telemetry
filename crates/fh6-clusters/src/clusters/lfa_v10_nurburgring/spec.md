**Category note.** The Lexus LFA's own instrument leads: its single central ring, its needle and its numerals, as step 1 finds them. This brief's ring is where the design starts, not where it ends.

A hubless TFT tach in the LFA spirit. One Ø480 circular-grain titanium ring, 24 px wide, floats on true black with no pivot and no face. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 190, in the ring centre; 16 slim LED bars sit on a straight shelf above it, 26 × 760 px. Rank 2: tabular Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 88, stacked directly under the gear. Rank 3: the needle is a light wedge from R 0.72 to R 0.95, a primary-colour core with a white edge, over Barlow Semi Condensed numerals on a scale rounded up from EngineMaxRpm. Above 180°/s it sheds up to four ghost wedges. Rank 4: a lap wing on the left (current, last and best lap in Titillium tabular figures) and a 4-step tyre-temperature quad on the right, both without glow. Rank 5: the crest, low inside the ring.

- **Signature moment:** Attack mode. After 0.4 s at ≥90% throttle above 70% rpm, the ring, numerals and needle rotate as one rigid group (slow spring, 363 ms) until the redline stands at 12. The numerals grow from cap 34 to cap 42 with a 500→700 weight crossfade. The ring's circular-grain highlight stays fixed to the light, as real circular grain would. After 3 s under 50% throttle the mode reverses; it never toggles during a gear change. From 92% of limiter rpm the face takes a steady red bath that ramps up to 30% alpha and never flashes. Only the LED shelf strobes. Opening the OSD glides the ring 160 px left on the 12 rad/s spring, which is the LFA's menu move.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the light wedge (needle spring with feed-forward), the LED thresholds and the red-bath ramp. The wedge's angular velocity feeds the afterimage, a fixed [f32; 4] ring buffer. throttle and rpm feed the attack-mode state machine (0.4 s in, 3 s out). EngineMaxRpm sets the scale end. speed and gear drive the centre stack. Current, last and best lap plus the lap number drive the lap wing. The four tyre temperatures drive the thermal quad: 4 discrete steps with a 150 ms crossfade, plus a count or position change so colour is never the only cue. Lateral G drives the light and the parallax (stack 0, ring 2, wings 4 px). The lap and tyre-temperature fields are not yet decoded by fh6-core; verify them against the golden packets.
- **References:** Lexus LFA TFT meter (moving ring, sport mode with bolder numerals and redline at the top, red bath near redline; co-designed with Polyphony Digital); Nürburgring Nordschleife lap-time culture; AiM/Bosch LED shift shelves
- **Phase 6 assets:** Circular-grain brushed titanium albedo/roughness with a tangential anisotropy map, with a conic-banding fallback. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 500, 700 and 800 (both weights are needed for the crossfade), Barlow Semi Condensed, Titillium Web and Michroma. The brand crest in mono white. Everything else is procedural.
- **Risk:** v1 drew an opaque panel of text lists with an off-palette yellow arc. Rotating the scale under a moving needle could also mislead for 363 ms. The rigid-group rotation keeps every reading correct, the mode needs 0.4 s of intent, and an OSD toggle can lock it off. The red bath must stay a steady ramp at ≤30% alpha and is counted in the red-area budget. Performance: the ring, wedge and four ghosts are SDF, est. 0.15–0.2 ms.
