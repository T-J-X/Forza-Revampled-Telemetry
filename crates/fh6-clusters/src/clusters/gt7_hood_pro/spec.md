# GT7 Hood Pro (`Gt7HoodPro`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 3 (★ Gt7HoodPro — build #2 · hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Modern Digital 2027 (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/modern-digital.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/modern-digital.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/modern-digital/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** hood · **Build order:** 2

Frameless 'gull-wing horizon' hung from one 1 px hairline (alpha 46, ±820 px). Twin mirrored ribbon tachs (7→24 px, power 1.6, primary→white core, comet orb) sweep from the wingtips (±820, −20) up to a crest at (0, −150). Idle sits at the tips and the car's shiftRpm at the crest. Titillium Web 600 numerals appear on the left wing only, with modern11 diamonds (28 px) 40 px above the crest. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 150, in a 250×210 oledBlack squircle with a light-driven 1 px rim and a feathered dimming pad (the hood scrim is 0%). Rank 2: Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 84, below it. Rank 4: thermal-step slip capsules on the pod's four corners make the pod read as the car; G puck on the right, THR/BRK on the left. Only ribbons, diamonds and gear glow.

- **Signature moment:** Apex fuse: as revs build the two comet orbs race inward. At the car's own shiftRpm they meet at the crest directly above the gear and smooth-union (k 10 px) into one steady 6×36 px vertical blade in `shift`, joining the ribbon to the centre diamond. This is the family's 'ribbon locks vertical' moment, timed with the outside-in diamond fill. At the limiter only the 28 px diamond strip strobes (10 Hz, 50% duty); the blade and the pod rim stay a steady `shift`. On an upshift the blade splits and both comets drop back outward to the new rpm on the `ribbon` spring with feed-forward, while the gear does the standard 1.12→1.0 entry.
- **Telemetry:** rpm current/idle/max plus car-profile shift and per-gear thresholds → twin ribbon positions, diamond stages, apex fuse, blade colour; gear → pod glyph (N at 72% ink, R in warning); speed → 3-cell tabular speed with ghost 8s and 0.6 hysteresis; tyre temps FL/FR/RL/RR → capsule thermal steps (shy °C numerals only outside the optimal band); tyre slip ratio / combined slip ×4 → capsule outline amber >0.08, red >0.18; acceleration x/z → G puck (felt force −a, gBall spring, 500 ms dot trail); throttle/brake → pedal capsules; lateral G → light azimuth on every rim and 0/2/4 px parallax; lap number/current/best → shy lap line above the pedal capsules, only when lap > 0.
- **References:** Gran Turismo 7 race-screen meter (Polyphony Digital); F1 2026 McLaren MCL40 progressive shift LEDs; Porsche 992.2 shift-at-12 track mode; Lucid Gravity bezel-free floating OLED
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (SDF ribbons, diamonds, squircle, capsules, G rings). Uses the shared MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600/800 and Titillium Web 600 and the 64×64 blue-noise dither tile. Retire assets/gt7_hood_pro_plate.png (the v1 opaque plate).
- **Risk:** Design: in the hood view (scrim 0%) the cluster sits over bright road and sky, so thin ribbons and the speed can fail the blur gate, and two converging tachs could read as duplicates. Fix: a feathered dimming pad (alpha ≤140) behind gear and speed only; the right-wing ribbon at 70% intensity with ticks but no numerals; diamonds kept 40 px clear of the crest. Perf: about 20 SDF draws in the 1760×400 clip plus half-res bloom of the emissive buffer ≈0.12 ms, with no full-screen pass.
