**A twist on the Skyline GT-R R34 and its Multi Function Display** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "for retro i want some nice twists on some nismo / skyline variants some retro and some modern"; in the game as the 2000 Nissan Skyline GT-R V-Spec II). Recalled, and to confirm in step 1 from the owner's manual's MFD chapter and Nismo's own material (the check of 2026-11-28 found only the 5.8-inch screen and its seven live readings, on Wikipedia): a cluster with the tachometer in the centre, scaled to 10,000 rpm with the red zone from 8,000, a speedometer on its left and a front-torque meter on its right; and the MFD, a 5.8-inch colour screen on top of the centre console that shows boost, throttle, injector duty and temperatures as numbers, bar graphs and a line graph over time, with a red zone the driver sets for each value. The twist: the MFD leaves the console and its pages float around the tachometer as the dash's own tiles.

Starting layout, detached parts. Rank 3: a Ø440 tachometer at (0, −20) as a rendered dial of the period, white numerals on a dark face and a red needle, scaled from `EngineMaxRpm`. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170) in a period liquid-crystal window at the dial's foot, and above the dial a straight row of 12 small lamps (740 × 28), filling outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 96) in its own window to the left of the dial, 400 px from the gear. Rank 4: two MFD tiles, each 330 × 240, at the outer left and right: the left holds three bar graphs with their numbers, the right holds one value as a line graph over the last 20 s. Rank 5: the tile captions in the MFD's own style and the GT-R badge as the logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** The driver's red zones. As on the real MFD, every bar has a red zone the driver sets (here in the OSD). When a value passes its mark the end of the bar turns `warning`, a small ▲ appears beside its number and the tile's caption inverts, all steady; when it drops back the bar leaves a peak tick behind. The line graph draws the same crossing as a shaded band, so a pull through the gears leaves a boost history the driver can read afterwards. At the shift rpm the lamp row is full; on the limiter only the lamp row strobes.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → tachometer needle (`needle` spring with feed-forward), lamp row and limiter; gear → the window; speed → tabular speed; boost, throttle and power → the three bars; any one of them, or longitudinal G → the line graph, in a fixed ring buffer of 600 samples; brake → an optional fourth bar. The real MFD's injector duty, oil, water, intake and exhaust temperatures and battery voltage are not in the telemetry, and neither is the torque that goes to the front axle: none of them is drawn, and the handover lists them as deliberate changes.
- **References:** Nissan Skyline GT-R (BNR34) owner's manual, MFD chapter; Nismo's MFD and combination-meter material and the Nismo Heritage Parts programme; owner photos and videos of the MFD's bar, line-graph and red-zone pages; `RetroCyber` in this catalog for period display materials
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the tachometer (ring, face, needle and hub cap) and the two tile housings as period plastics with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: the grain of a late-1990s liquid-crystal panel with its backlight bleed, and the dial-face print. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800, Barlow Semi Condensed 500 and a period-style label face from the OFL pack. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** The MFD's real look cannot be rebuilt from memory: no tile is designed before its real page is in `RESEARCH.md`. A low-resolution period screen drawn at 4K can look like a bug: keep the pixel grid as a visible, deliberate texture and the numbers crisp above it. The gear window is small on the real car: here it must still reach cap 150 or more and win the glance. Red zones use `warning`, so the dial's own red needle and band stay within the red budget. Perf: one dial plate, two tiles and a 600-point line in chunks of 8, est. 0.25 ms.
