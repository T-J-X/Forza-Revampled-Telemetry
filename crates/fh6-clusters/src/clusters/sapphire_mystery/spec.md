# Sapphire Mystery (`SapphireMystery`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 37 (SapphireMystery — build #38 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Classic Analog (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/classic-analog.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/classic-analog.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/classic-analog/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/classic-analog/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 38

A mystery dial made for a display that is itself transparent: no face, no ring and no visible pivot. Lume indices hang over the live cockpit on three parallax layers. The rpm hand floats, its root lost in an invisible Ø520 sapphire disc (alpha 28) that shows only as a 1 px fresnel crescent where the G-driven light grazes it. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 700 gear, cap 190, in a domed sapphire cabochon (Ø230, alpha 44) that lenses the indices behind it; 13 floating lume dots form the shift strip, 24 × 740 px. Rank 2: tabular Saira 300 speed, cap 100, floating under the cabochon on a dimming pad. Rank 3: a primary-colour lance with a lume core and a counterweight tail; numerals appear only at the quarter marks and at the shift rpm, which sits at 12. Rank 4: a ringless G-dot under the speed. Rank 5: one Michroma name, cap 12.

- **Signature moment:** Lume wake. The floating hand charges every index it passes. Each one glows lume green and decays with τ = 400 ms, so a pull leaves a phosphor wake up the dial and a lift-off leaves one coming down. At constant rpm the wake dies and only the two indices bracketing the tip stay lit, so idle is still. At the shift rpm the 12 o'clock index charges in the shift colour instead. At the limiter only the floating dot strip strobes. The invisible disc's 1 px rim then lights steady in the shift colour, the only moment the whole crystal can be seen.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the floating hand (needle spring with feed-forward), the per-index charge (a fixed [f32; 48] array with exponential decay from FrameInput dt, no allocation) and the dot thresholds. gear and speed drive the cabochon glyph and the floating digits. Lateral G drives the light azimuth (crescent position, fresnel arc) and the 0/2/4 px parallax of the index layers. G lat/long drive the G-dot. OemDashOpaquePct sets the dimming pad behind gear and speed (alpha ≤140 × (1 − scrim)). All of these fields are already decoded.
- **References:** Mystery clocks (hands carried on transparent rotating crystal discs); AR-coated domed sapphire crystals; Lucid Gravity's floating bezel-free OLED; Super-LumiNova phosphorescence
- **Phase 6 assets:** A sapphire-dome normal/refraction pair and a soft caustic crescent texture, with an analytic dome and crescent as the procedural fallback; a 64×64 blue-noise dither tile for the alpha-28 disc; MSDF atlases for Saira 300, Saira Condensed 700, Barlow Semi Condensed and Michroma.
- **Risk:** With no face or ring, the dial can lose its frame of reference over a busy scene at 0% scrim. Keep the quarter numerals and the dimming pad, cap the wake at 3 lit indices behind the hand, and require the blur gate on a C1 capture (gear ≥7:1, speed ≥4.5:1). It must also stay clearly apart from Liquid Glass: no frost, no light running round the rims, and glass used only as the invisible carrier. Performance: one disc with a 40 px lensing band, one cabochon and 48 instanced indices, est. 0.15–0.2 ms, with dither against banding.
