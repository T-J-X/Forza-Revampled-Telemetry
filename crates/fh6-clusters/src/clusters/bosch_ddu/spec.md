# Bosch DDU (`BoschDdu`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 12 (★ BoschDdu — build #4 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Race Dash (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/race-dash.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/race-dash.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/race-dash/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/race-dash/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 4

Fixes the hero's faults: dead lamps, boxed tiles, a fixed 0–9 scale. An open-bottom U of 2×2 carbon twill, 14 px wide, 1400×540, with a deep 28 px 45° chamfer whose lit flank (55%) and shadow flank (18%) follow `light`; four Allen bolts. Inside: a true-black cavity under bonded AR cover glass, an 18/255 crescent, 1 px `glass` AR rim tint. Rank 1: ten 36 px microprism pods over a `race` 800 gear at cap 240. Rank 2: speed, cap 112, left. Rank 3: 45-segment bar numbered from the car's max rpm. Rank 4: signed long-G bar above the gear, thermal tyre quad below. Corner-ticked tiles, Titillium Web labels; three data-bound alarm lamps per side (16 LEDs in all, the CARTEK spirit), lit without bloom.

- **Signature moment:** The family signature, owned here: pods light in the same frame each per-car, per-gear threshold is crossed, green → amber → red, outer pair first, the last landing on the centre pod above the gear; at shift rpm all ten take `shift` (#2F6BFF default) and at the limiter only the 36 px pod strip strobes at 10 Hz, 50% duty. BoschDdu's one steady cue: the upper carbon chamfer takes a non-emissive `shift` reflection ramped 0→60% over 150 ms, as if the pods spill onto the bevel; it never flashes. Event page: crossing the line swaps the right tiles to LAST / Δ BEST for 4 s with a stepped 150 ms crossfade.
- **Telemetry:** current_engine_rpm + engine_max_rpm + car-profile shift/limiter rpm → pods, strobe, 45-segment bar and its numerals; gear → hero glyph; speed_ms → tabular mph/km/h with 0.6 hysteresis; acceleration_z → signed long-G bar; acceleration_x → light azimuth and 0/2/4 px parallax; tyre temps ×4 → thermal steps; lap number, current/last/best lap, race position → tiles (free roam: distance, boost_psi, power_watts); lamps: LIM (rpm ≥ limiter), FUEL (<10%), TYRE (any tyre overheated), SLIP (combined slip), and TC / ABS synthesised from driven-wheel slip ratio per drivetrain_type with throttle >0.2 or brake >0.3 (thresholds prop., labelled derived).
- **References:** Bosch Motorsport DDU 10/11 (10 top + side RGB lamps, 6-step brightness, event pages); Cosworth CCW Mk3 (10 shift + 6 alarm); MoTeC SLM-C (warnings outrank shift stages); Porsche 963 top light bar
- **Phase 6 assets:** Carbon twill albedo+normal tile (12 px weave, 512², seamless) with the 2×2 procedural fallback; microprism pod-lens normal map 128² (procedural concentric-ridge fallback); Allen bolt normal sprite 64² (SDF fallback); 128² blue-noise dither tile; MSDF Saira Condensed 700/800 and Titillium Web 600 with a tnum check.
- **Risk:** Design: sliding back into the hero's dense boxed rectangle where ranks 2–5 share one weight. Avoid: corner-tick tiles, 3 lamps per side, static structure ≤20% luminance, race/zen tiers. Perf: bloom only on pods, gear and bar inside a 1400×600 scissor (8 levels from 256 px) ≈0.12 ms; twill + chamfer + glass in one SDF pass ≈0.03 ms; ≈0.25 ms total. Blockers: FH6TelemetryData today lacks tyre temps, slip ratio, combined slip, fuel and lap fields (extend the decoder against golden packets); gear_string() allocates a String, so use a static glyph table; COMPONENTS §14 lets lit alarms glow, but the rank rule wins (no bloom).
