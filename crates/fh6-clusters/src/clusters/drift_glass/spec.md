**Category note.** A drift scoring tool. The helmet visor is its housing and stays, built by absorption on the kit's glass and not by a flat alpha. From the Motorsport category it takes exact data and shift lights that behave like the real hardware; it keeps its own family's materials.

'Smoked visor': one 1240×300 Tinted glass visor (alpha 56–64, glassTint) with a helmet-curved top and tapered flanks; its lower 40 px feather to zero. Two 1 px creases split it into three facets, so the G-driven light swaps brightness between them in corners. It has a polished 1 px top rim and a 2 px edge-glow lip. Rank 1: outsideIn15 pills (24 px) above the visor, and a Saira Condensed 800 gear, cap 200, in a faceted hexagonal smoked lozenge at X 1920. Rank 2: Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 112, 300 px right. Rank 3: a 12 px light pipe inside the visor's top edge, primary→white meniscus. Rank 4: a 180° drift fan under the gear. Shy Titillium labels.

- **Signature moment:** Slip wedge with peak scribe: above 8° slip, a smoked wedge snaps open in the fan from the travel-direction axis to the body heading. Its colour steps by band: 8–20° primary, 20–40° accent, >40° caution, with a 150 ms crossfade. A 1 px steer tick on the fan rim shows counter-steer against the wedge, and the G comet rides the same pivot. The slide's peak angle leaves a scribed tick held for 2 s; once the slide ends (β < 4° for 300 ms), 'PEAK 41°' shows for 1.5 s. At the limiter only the pill strip strobes, and the visor lip warms to a steady `shift`.
- **Telemetry:** local velocity x/z → β = atan2(v_lat, v_long), hidden below 5 m/s, with 8°/4° hysteresis; angular velocity y → wedge feed-forward; steer (normalised, no wheel-angle claim) → counter-steer tick; acceleration x/z → G comet direction and length (gBall spring); rear tyre combined slip → wedge edge amber; handbrake flag → shy HB label; rpm/idle/max + shift → light pipe and pills; gear; speed; throttle → meniscus intensity; lateral G → light azimuth (facet swap) and 0/2/4 px parallax.
- **References:** D1 Grand Prix DOSS drift-angle scoring overlays; tinted race-helmet visors; Apple iOS 26.1 Tinted Liquid Glass; Mercedes AR-HUD sparse symbology
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: faceted visor SDF, tint, frost noise (1.4 px, 3.5%) and the blue-noise dither tile; shared MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600/800 and Titillium Web 600.
- **Risk:** Design: Tinted glass at alpha 64 over a bright scene can read as a grey box (OLED rule 7), and a wedge flickering at slide transitions would distract. Fix: tint only inside the facets, with the 40 px feather and no fill below the visor; no live blur of game pixels (smoke = tint + frost noise + rims); β hysteresis and the `snap` spring with feed-forward. Perf: visor and fan are analytic in one 1240×520 clipped pass plus instanced pills ≈0.1 ms.
