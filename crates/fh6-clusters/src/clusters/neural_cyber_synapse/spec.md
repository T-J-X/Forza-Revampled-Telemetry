# Neural Cyber Synapse (`NeuralCyberSynapse`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 23 (NeuralCyberSynapse — build #23 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 23

A living neuron. The gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 210) is the soma: a 260 px cell body with a 1 px membrane rim lit by the G light. A dendritic tree branches upward 1 -> 2 -> 4 -> 8 -> 16, Bezier fibres tapering 10 -> 2 px across 1300 px. Rank 3: the trunk and four branch levels are the tach, lighting root-outward up to 75% of shift rpm; dark fibres sit at alpha 22. Rank 1: the 16 terminal boutons form a straight 30 px shift strip on the crown, filling outside-in. Rank 2: Saira 200 speed (cap 100) right of the soma, beside the trunk. Rank 4: an unglowing three-channel EEG raster (throttle, brake, steer) under speed and soma. Fibres are primary; the signal front is primary lifted to white.

- **Signature moment:** The action potential climbs the tree. Rpm drives a depolarisation front from the soma up through the branch levels in the same frame, with a 2-node bright spike at the front whose height scales with throttle. From 75% the signal lands in the boutons outside-in, so tach and shift lights are one continuous gesture ending above the gear. Upshift: 12 vesicle particles burst from the soma rim for 150 ms (event only). Limiter: the boutons strobe at 10 Hz and the soma membrane thickens to 3 px in accent, steady, replacing the old flashing 'lightning' idea.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm sets the front height in the tree and the bouton fill (per-car, per-gear thresholds). Gear drives the soma glyph; this replaces the v1 hardcoded '7'. Speed drives the speed. Accel sets the spike amplitude and EEG channel 1, Brake EEG channel 2 (warning), and Steer EEG channel 3 (signed). Clutch opens a gap in the soma rim. Felt lateral G sets the light azimuth and a 2 px lean of the tree. The EEG traces scroll in time but stay flat under constant input, so idle is visually still.
- **References:** Confocal micrographs of Purkinje neurons and Cajal's public-domain ink drawings; textbook saltatory-conduction diagrams; Tron: Legacy procedural line work (Digital Domain); Oblivion GFX rationed greeble (GMUNK)
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: the tree is a fixed, symmetric Bezier layout computed once at load and drawn as SDF capsules. An optional 256x256 membrane noise for the soma interior (gradient fallback). Saira and Chakra Petch MSDF atlases.
- **Risk:** Organic branching can read as noise at 220 mph. The tree is strictly symmetric with fixed levels, the 16 boutons stay a straight 30 px strip, and fibres beyond the front sit at alpha 22. Cost: 31 fibres and 16 pods instanced as SDF capsules plus three 512-point polylines, about 0.15 ms, with bloom on the front, boutons and gear only.
