**The cockpit sibling of `Gt7HoodPro`** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "we already have Gt7HoodPro nearly finished id like some other variants of this as its just clean"). `Gt7HoodPro` exists only in the hood view. This dash is the same clean instrument for the cockpit view, so that pressing RB between cockpit and hood changes the camera and not the language. Start from the finished `Gt7HoodPro` (its `mod.rs`, `RESEARCH.md` and `HANDOVER.md`) and from Gran Turismo 7's own race-screen meter, which step 1 researches again for the cockpit and chase views; the layout below is the starting point.

Starting layout, frameless and detached, on the 1440 × 560 binnacle. Rank 3: the two mirrored ribbon tachs of the hood dash rise from the lower outer corners at (±690, +170) and meet in a pointed arch at (0, −270), idle at the feet and the car's shift rpm at the crest, numerals on the left limb only. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 190) in an `oledBlack` squircle at (0, −70) under the crest, with 11 diamonds (28 px, 760 px span) on a straight line above the arch's shoulders. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira Condensed 600, cap 100) under the gear. Rank 4: inside the arch's left flank the pedal capsules, inside its right flank the G puck, each small and without glow; a shy lap line under the speed. Only the ribbons, the diamonds and the gear glow.

- **Signature moment:** The apex fuse, shared with the hood dash: the two comet heads climb the arch and meet at the crest at the car's own shift rpm, where they fuse into one steady vertical blade in `shift` that points down at the gear. On the limiter only the diamond line strobes. On an upshift the blade splits and both comets drop back down their limbs on the `ribbon` spring. What is new here is the handover: on a change of view the gear, the speed and the diamonds hold their size and order, so the eye finds them where the other view left them.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max with the car's per-gear thresholds → ribbons, diamonds, fuse and limiter; gear → squircle numeral (N at 72% ink, R in `warning`); speed → tabular speed with ghost digits; throttle and brake → pedal capsules; acceleration → G puck on the `gBall` spring with a 500 ms trail; lateral G → the light on every rim; speed and timestamp → the acceleration timers on the lap line. Tyre slip and tyre temperature are not verified: the corner capsules of the hood dash appear here only after those fields are verified in the real game, and until then the flanks hold pedals and G.
- **References:** Gran Turismo 7 race-screen meter in the cockpit and chase views (Polyphony Digital); `Gt7HoodPro` and `Gt7ModularTripod` in this catalog; Porsche 992.2 shift-at-12 track mode; Formula 1 progressive shift lights
- **Phase 6 assets:** Shares the hood dash's parts: compose them from `common/` and ask the coordinator for a shared helper where one is missing; never copy its module. Blender: the gear squircle's rim and the diamond caps relit for the cockpit, plus the arch's two feet as machined end caps. ComfyUI: the fine matrix under the ribbons that the hood dash uses, re-made at the cockpit scale. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 600 and 800 and Titillium Web 600.
- **Risk:** A second dash in the same language can be a copy: the arch, the cockpit proportions and the handover between views are its reasons to exist, and the closing review compares the two side by side. A tall arch puts the ribbons close to the gear: keep 60 px of true black between the ribbon and the squircle, and the right limb at 70% intensity so the two limbs do not read as two tachs. In the cockpit the scrim hides the game's dash, so no dimming pad is needed. Perf: the hood dash's draws re-placed, est. 0.12 ms.
