**Real display first** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "multi versions of AMG MBUX i love this … each version i want as close as possible"). This dash is the **Supersport** menu of the second-generation MBUX driver display: the 12.3-inch screen of the Mercedes-AMG GT (C192), SL (R232) and C 63 S E Performance. What the sources say (owner's-manual supplements and press texts, read 2026-11-28): the screen has three parts, *current torque* on the left, a *centre display content* (the manual's example is the gear display) and *current power output* on the right; the centre content is chosen from a vertical menu (gear, G-force, engine data, temperatures, set-up); in manual mode the gear carries a yellow `M`; a refused shift lights segments red. Mercedes describes the first-generation Supersport as "a central, round rev counter" with "additional information presented in the form of bars to the left and right", drawn "with a three-dimensional perspective" so that "they reach far into the background to an artificial horizon", plus "a prominent prompt to change up a gear ('shift light')". **Not in any text source, so take it from frame grabs in step 1:** whether the second-generation centre is still a round rev counter, where the digital speed sits, the scale end and red area, the shape of the torque and power elements, the colours and the typeface. Where the frames and this brief disagree, the frames win.

Starting layout, three detached parts on true black with no housing (the real screen's bezel is not part of the look). Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 190) at (0, −40) inside a Ø440 rev counter drawn with depth, a yellow `M` tag beside it, and an upshift bar of 12 cells (760 × 30) above the dial crown, filling outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira 300, cap 96) at the dial top, 150 px above the gear. Rank 3: the rev counter's lit arc, numbered from `EngineMaxRpm`. Rank 4: two wings that sweep away from the dial to an artificial horizon at y −60, torque on the left and power on the right, each a perspective bar with a tabular value and a peak-hold tick. Rank 5: micro-labels and the AMG logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** The dyno in the wings. Through a pull the torque wing rises, peaks and falls back while the power wing keeps climbing, and each leaves a hold tick at its maximum, so the driver sees the engine's two peaks every time. At the shift rpm the upshift bar closes on its centre and the rev counter's rim holds a steady `shift` colour; on the limiter only the bar strobes. On an upshift both wings collapse toward the dial on the `fast` spring and re-open at the new rpm, and the gear enters at 1.12 → 1.0.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max → rev counter, upshift bar and limiter; gear → centre numeral, with the `M` tag always on (TJX drives manual); speed → tabular speed; power → right wing, in `signals.power_unit`; torque is not a verified field, so the left wing shows power ÷ angular engine speed (τ = P·60 / (2π·rpm), rpm clamped to idle) and the handover says so; boost → the engine-data centre page; acceleration → the G-force centre page. The centre page is the driver's choice in the OSD (`config.extra` key `AmgMbuxSupersportCentre`: gear, G-force or engine data); nothing pages by itself. Temperatures and the set-up page have no telemetry and are left out.
