- **Family:** Fluid / Generative (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/)

Ferrofluid is black, so on OLED only its highlights exist: bodies are true #000 and form is drawn purely by G-moved specular env-band reflections and 1 px rims. A ferrofluid pool runs along a 1300 px crown arc (rise 50, apex y −200); Rosensweig spikes stand up along it from the left to the rpm front (rank 3), the lead spike tallest with a primary-tinted ridge. The gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200, ink) is a polished pole piece at (0,−20) whose outline bristles with micro-spikes; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 112) at (+290,−10). Twelve 30 px pole caps are the shift strip at y −292. Under gear and speed, four ferro-drops in a car footprint read tyre slip.

- **Signature moment:** A gear change is a field-collapse wave. A single dip travels left to right through the spike row: each spike slumps to 30% and recovers on the `fast` spring. At the same time the micro-spike fringe crawls off the old gear outline onto the new one by smooth-union migration over 168 ms. The glyph swaps in frame 1 and the rpm front stays exact throughout. The spike lattice warps, compressing toward the front like a field gradient. Limiter: the lead spikes fuse into one tall crown spike at the redline end, lit steadily in `shift`; only the pole-cap strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** rpm → spike-front position (`ribbon` spring with feed-forward; the front is exact) and lattice compression toward it. throttle → field current = spike height (40–100%, through the `snap` spring). boost → spike sharpness (apex angle 38° → 18°). gLat → spike lean (shear on `fluidSlosh`); gLong → foreshortening under braking. Per-gear thresholds → pole caps. gear → pole-piece glyph plus the collapse wave. speed → tabular. Per-wheel combined slip (packet field, decoder extension needed; tyre slip angles, which are decoded, as the interim input) → spikes erupting on each ferro-drop (0 at grip, up to 7), with `caution`/`warning` outline steps at 0.08/0.18. Tyre temperatures (decoder extension) → 4-step thermal tint on each drop's 1 px rim.
- **References:** Sachiko Kodama's ferrofluid sculptures ('Protrude, Flow', 2001); the Rosensweig normal-field instability lab demonstration; spec-only photography of piano-black automotive trim; the family hero frames for the env-band language.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Ferrofluid spike flipbook (32 frames, 512²) with a normal map as the budget fallback. A dark-studio 256×1 env LUT with two softbox highlights that move with the G-light (procedural fallback: token `liquidMetal` env bands × 0.6). 128² blue-noise. Exo 2 MSDF. Spikes themselves are procedural: cone SDF lattice smooth-unioned with the pool.
- **Risk:** Black on black: spec-only shading vanishes when the light leaves the softboxes. Every cone keeps a 1 px rim at alpha ≥60, and the gear is an ink-filled pole piece, never a black ferro glyph (v1 shipped an opaque black box with a tiny gear). Spikes boil on noisy throttle, so throttle passes the `snap` spring with a 2% deadband. Perf: 64 cone SDFs smooth-unioned in a 1300×160 clip plus 4 drop SDFs, about 0.04 ms. Hood [4] fit: the crown widens to 1600 px and flattens to rise 24, with gear compact cap 150.
