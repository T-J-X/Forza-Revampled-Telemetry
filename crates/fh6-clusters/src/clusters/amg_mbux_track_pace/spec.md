**Real display first** (TJX, 2026-11-28: each AMG MBUX version "as close as possible"). This dash is the **Track Pace** menu of the second-generation MBUX driver display, the screen that goes with AMG TRACK PACE, the car's data logger. What the sources say: the screen has three parts, a *G-meter* on the left, a *centre display content* (the manual's example is the gear display) and a *telemetry display* on the right (the example is the selected race track); the G-meter shows lateral and longitudinal force in a coordinate system and "the maximum values will be shown in red"; TRACK PACE records more than 80 values ten times a second, shows lap and sector times, and marks them "in green or red" against the reference lap; it also times acceleration, the quarter mile and braking; timing starts when the car crosses the start line. **Take from frame grabs in step 1:** the drawn form of the G-meter, how lap time, delta and sectors are laid out in the cluster, whether a rev band is part of the menu, the colours and the typeface.

Starting layout, three detached parts. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 200) at (0, −50) with the yellow `M` tag, under an upshift bar of 12 cells (760 × 30) at y −290, filling outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Saira 300, cap 96) under the gear. Rank 3: a slim rev band across the top of the centre part. Rank 4, left: a Ø300 G-meter with rings at 0.5 g, a cross-hair, a dot on the `gBall` spring and the maxima held as ticks with their values. Rank 4, right: the telemetry part, 400 × 300, with the driven line as a heading-up map, the current lap, the last and best lap, and the delta. Hood view: the same three parts spread across 1760 × 400 with the map dropped to a delta bar.

- **Signature moment:** The delta swing. As the car closes a lap the time freezes for 2 s, the delta flips in with its sign and an up or down arrow, and the finished lap becomes the reference line on the map, with a ghost dot showing where the best lap was at the same elapsed time. Ahead is `go`, behind is `warning`, always with the sign and the arrow, so colour is never the only cue. As the real car tints its cabin light, the kit's ambient wash leans green or red with the delta, as a steady ramp under 20% that never flashes and can be switched off. On the limiter only the upshift bar strobes.
- **Telemetry:** acceleration → G-meter and its held maxima; gear, speed, rpm → the centre part; speed and the packet timestamp → the acceleration timers (0 to 100 km/h, 0 to 200, the quarter mile by integrating speed, and braking from 100), which use verified fields only and ship first. The game's lap and sector fields are not decoded, so the dash times laps itself: world position and the timestamp give the driven line, and a lap closes when the line returns to its own start within 15 m on the same heading. World position is decoded but not covered by the golden packets: verify it in the real game before the map and the lap timer ship (97% rule) and say so in the handover. The best lap's samples live in a fixed ring buffer; nothing allocates.
- **References:** MBUSA owner's-manual supplements for the AMG C-Class and CLE (the Track Pace figure and its legend); Mercedes-AMG TRACK PACE press and manual texts; owner videos of TRACK PACE on the AMG GT and C 63 for frame grabs; `AmgMbuxSupersport` in this catalog, whose parts and palette it shares
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: the G-meter's ring and lens as a rendered part with three light azimuths. ComfyUI: the dark panel material the frame grabs show behind the telemetry part. MSDF atlases for Saira Condensed 800, Saira 300 and Titillium Web 600. Procedural fallback for every part.
- **Risk:** The real function depends on stored circuits and the game has none, so the lap timer must never claim more than it knows: label the line TRAIL and the timer LAP only after a closed lap, and show dashes until then. Green and red deltas fail for colour-blind drivers unless the sign and the arrow carry the same message. Three busy parts can out-shout gear and speed: the side parts stay at rank 4 luminance with no bloom. Perf: the map is a polyline of at most 240 points drawn in chunks of 8, est. 0.2 ms.
