# Dichroic Louvre (`DichroicLouvre`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 32 (DichroicLouvre — build #37 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 37

A kinetic arch of 28 vertical glass fins (18×96 px) on a 200° arc (semi-axes 560×200) around the gear. Each fin turns on its own axis. Edge-on it is a single 1 px rim line, so OLED pixels stay off; face-on it is a lit dichroic pane in primary, shading to accent only in a grazing fresnel sheen. Rank 3: fins turn face-on in order as rpm passes each fin's threshold. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 200) on a dimming pad at the arch's centre, plus a 760×40 strip of 15 pods above the apex. Rank 2: speed left of the gear, Saira 300 cap 104, ghost 8s. Rank 4: four tyre fins in a car footprint under the gear and speed. Titillium Web labels.

- **Signature moment:** Shift-point snap: when the last fin turns face-on at the shift rpm, the whole arch steps in one frame from primary to the shift colour. It becomes a steady dichroic wall that never flashes, while the 15-pod strip strobes at 10 Hz at the limiter. As rpm drops on an upshift, the fins roll back edge-on from the right in a wave. The wave is only the rpm falling (needle spring, no added delay). In a corner, the accent sheen slides across the fin faces with the G-driven light.
- **Telemetry:** rpm + per-gear thresholds → each fin's angle θ = 90°·(1 − clamp((rpm − r_i)/band)) on the ribbon spring with feed-forward, and the pod strip. Gear and speed → hero glyphs. Lateral G → light azimuth, which sets where the accent sheen sits on the fins. Throttle → face brightness 70→100%. Per-wheel slip angle → each tyre fin's yaw; tyre temps → its thermal step; slip ratio → amber/red fin rim. Brake → caution rim on the front tyre fins. Native fields only; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Architectural dichroic-glass fin installations that transmit and reflect two colours by angle; kinetic louvre facades; flip-disc and split-flap displays as rotating-element readouts; Apple Liquid Glass specular following geometry (WWDC25).
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only. Fins are analytic oriented quads (projected width w·/cos θ/) with a two-role thin-film shader, and a 256-entry dichroic ramp LUT is baked from the palette at load. MSDF atlases: Saira Condensed, Saira, Titillium Web.
- **Risk:** Design risk: at high rpm, 28 face-on fins make a wall of light that out-shouts the gear and breaks the colour budget. To avoid it: fin faces at ≤45% intensity with bloom on fin edges only, accent limited to a ≤10% fresnel sheen, and a dimming pad that keeps the gear at ≥7:1. Edge-on fins stay at hairline alpha. Performance: 32 instanced quads in one pass, plus bloom. Estimate ≈0.12 ms.
