# Fibre-Optic Corona (`FibreOpticCorona`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 33 (FibreOpticCorona — build #48 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Liquid Glass (Design Council 3A), spec [docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/liquid-glass.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/liquid-glass/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 48

96 drawn-glass fibres fan out of a polished ferrule that holds the gear. The 2 px cores sit unlit at alpha 30 and curve up and out as cubic Béziers to tips on a 210° elliptical arc (600×260). Rank 3: fibres light in rpm order, carrying light visibly from the ferrule to a polished bead tip (r 6); white is reserved for the tips. Rank 1: the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 200) on the 240 px ferrule face, a sapphire disc with a 1 px rim, plus a 15-pod strip (40 px) above the arc apex. Rank 2: speed left of the ferrule, Saira 300 cap 112, ghost 8s. Rank 4: a 37-cell close-packed fibre faceplate under the ferrule. Titillium Web labels.

- **Signature moment:** Upshift pulse: a bright packet leaves the ferrule rim and runs out along every lit fibre at 4000 px/s. The fibres have equal optical length, so all the tips flash together for one frame at 60%. A downshift sends the packet inward, landing on the gear. At the limiter only the pod strip strobes at 10 Hz; the lit fibres stay steady and their tips warm to the shift colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm + per-gear thresholds → number of lit fibres and the pod strip. Throttle → brightness gradient along each lit fibre (core 60→100%). Gear → ferrule glyph and pulse direction. Speed → tabular field. Acceleration x/z (felt force) → the lit faceplate cell, with its neighbours at 30%. Tyre combined slip → the lit cell spreads to its 6 neighbours as grip goes. Lateral G → light azimuth on the ferrule rim. Brake → caution on the faceplate rim. Native fields only; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Fibre-optic faceplates and fibre-optic lamps; Tron: Legacy's procedural line work under a strict rulebook (GMUNK); Bugatti Tourbillon centre dial with satellites; the WWDC25 Liquid Glass glow that starts at a contact point and spreads.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only. The 96 Bézier fibres are baked at load into a distance + arc-length texture (RG16F over the clip), so drawing them costs one fetch per pixel. Also a faceplate SDF. MSDF atlases: Saira, Saira Condensed, Titillium Web.
- **Risk:** Design risk: 96 hairlines read as greeble and moiré on WRGB, and lit fibres break the colour budget. To avoid it: tip spacing ≥14 px, 2 px cores with SDF AA, unlit fibres at ≤12% luminance, and lit fibres in primary only with white on the tips; the texture is rebaked only on a scale change. Performance: one fetch pass over the clip, plus bloom on ranks 1–3. Estimate ≈0.16 ms.
