# Aero Plasma Blade (`AeroPlasmaBlade`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 25 (AeroPlasmaBlade — build #25 · hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Sci-Fi (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/sci-fi.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/sci-fi.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/sci-fi/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** hood · **Build order:** 25

A delta-wing planform seen from above, 1700x380 on the hood: apex up at X=1920, 55 deg leading-edge sweep, tips low on both sides. The skin is never filled; 1 px rims and three spar hairlines draw it, lit by the G light. Rank 3: both leading edges carry a dielectric-discharge line (6 px core, neon halo) that ignites from the apex to the tips in 20 steps per side, with 1000-rpm ticks along the edge. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 800 gear (cap 190) in the fuselage under the apex, with a 34 px 16-pod crown strip. Rank 2: B612 Mono speed (cap 96) on the spine below. Rank 4: nine unglowing hairline streamlines across the chord.

- **Signature moment:** Leading-edge ignition. Rpm lights the discharge from the apex to the tips. At the shift rpm both tips spin up steady wingtip-vortex spirals in the shift colour, while only the crown strip strobes at 10 Hz. In a slide the nine streamlines swing to the car's real sideslip angle beta and peel off the leeward tip as dashed separated flow, so a drift reads as airflow. Upshift: the discharge falls back to the new rpm and the apex chevron gives a 1-frame 60% rim flash.
- **Telemetry:** CurrentEngineRpm drives the discharge length and the strip. Gear drives the glyph and Speed the B612 speed. VelocityX/Z give beta = atan2(v_lat, v_long) for the streamline angle, hidden below 5 m/s with a 120 ms fade. DistanceTraveled advects the streamline dashes. NormalizedSuspensionTravel left and right pairs droop each tip by up to 12 px as an aero-load proxy, labelled as such. Front versus rear TireSlipAngle picks which side separates (understeer or oversteer). Boost drives the discharge core brightness. Felt lateral G sets the light azimuth.
- **References:** Dielectric-barrier-discharge plasma-actuator flow-control research footage; wind-tunnel smoke-streamline photography; 1960s ogival-delta supersonic planform drawings; aviation HUD stroke conventions (2 px core, 1 px AA)
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: planform, discharge, streamlines and vortex spirals are SDF. B612 Mono and Saira Condensed MSDF atlases.
- **Risk:** A wide planform can read as decoration, and a diagonal tach is slower to read than an arc. The gear stays on X=1920, the skin is never filled, the discharge steps are discrete, and 1000-rpm ticks sit on each edge. Hood-only placement keeps the planform from crowding the cockpit binnacle. Cost: up to 60 SDF segments plus 9 polylines, with bloom on the discharge, gear and strip only, about 0.1 ms.
