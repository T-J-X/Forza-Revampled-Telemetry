**Category note.** The lattice floor and the well are geometry placed in the world under the car; no glass panes. The floor is a Gaussian well ([Real geometry and maths](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md#real-geometry-and-maths)), and the tyre slip and suspension travel this brief reads are not verified yet.

Spacetime instead of scenery. A perspective lattice floor (24 px pitch at the near edge, 2 px hairlines at alpha 46, dissolving to black over 120 px) is bent into a Gaussian well beneath the gear by the force the driver feels. The car is a 28 px light puck at the well's centre, and its predicted path is a geodesic rolling across the warped grid. Z1: twelve 20 px round photon pods on a straight strip at y −292, filling outside-in. Gear (Saira Condensed 800, cap 180) floats above the well at (0, −160), speed left (Saira Light, cap 112), boost and lap right (Titillium). Rank 3 is an event-horizon ring, the well's rim on the floor, filling from its far point toward the near point on both sides.

- **Signature moment:** The grip horizon. The well's depth tracks /G/ and its centre slides toward the felt force (half a σ per g). When any tyre's combined slip exceeds 1.0, the rim tears: grid lines crossing it break open on the sliding side for as long as the slide lasts, then heal on the fast spring when grip returns. At the shift rpm the ring closes at its near point in the shift colour. At the limiter only the pod strip strobes 10 Hz while the closed ring holds steady.
- **Telemetry:** Local accel x/z (felt force = −a) → well offset and depth on the gBall spring; combined tyre slip ×4 → rim-tear side and length; angular velocity y / max(v, 3 m/s) → geodesic curvature; local velocity → β notch on the puck; speed → grid lines passing at true ground speed (still when stopped); normalised suspension travel ×4 → puck tilt; rpm → pods and event-horizon ring; gear and speed → hero readouts; boost and lap → right readouts. Everything is derived from the packet; no world is synthesised.
- **References:** Flamm's paraboloid and rubber-sheet embedding diagrams of curved spacetime; Oblivion (2013) GMUNK lattice discipline; Tesla FSD ego-centred chase framing
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (a 64×32 vertex grid displaced in the vertex shader, SDF puck, pods and ring); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600
- **Risk:** Constant grid motion and warping can feel busy or nauseating and pull the eye from ranks 1–2. Keep the grid at hairline alpha ≤46, cap the well depth at 60 px on screen, fade the lattice beyond 1.5 s of travel, and never write the floor to bloom. The ring is rank 3 but in perspective, so parametrise its fill by angle on the ground ellipse and taper it from 6 px near to 3 px far. Fine scrolling lines can moiré on WOLED: use analytic fwidth AA and keep the 24 px minimum pitch. GPU <0.07 ms.
