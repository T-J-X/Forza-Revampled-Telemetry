**Category note.** Recreates the Sporty style of the driver display that sits beside the MBUX Superscreen glass ([The five AMG dashes](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md#the-five-amg-dashes)). Where this older brief invents (the convex gear lens, the 3D trail map), the real display wins, and the driven-line map belongs to `AmgMbuxTrackPace`.

'Pillar-to-pillar black glass': a 1400×320 curved superscreen with no fill, implied only by a light-driven 1 px top rim, a 40% bottom rim and a 60 px reflection band (alpha 10) that slides across the 'curved' sheet with lateral G. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 700 gear, cap 200, inside a Ø280 convex glass lens (fresnel rim, crescent) at X 1920, with modern11 diamonds above. Rank 2: Saira 200 light speed, cap 112, 360 px left, ghost 8s. Rank 3: a 1120×18 px extruded performance bar along the band top. Its bevelled glass cells are lit by the virtual light and fill in primary, with a phase-coloured end cap. Rank 4: a 640×180 3D trail map under the lens. Shy Titillium power/torque/boost tiles sit right.

- **Signature moment:** Phase-painted contrail: under the gear lens, the last 120 world-position trail points rise as a 3D light curtain behind a brand-tinted ego chevron, seen heading-up from an orbit camera pitched 55° down. Curtain height is elevation change. Each point is coloured by the phase recorded there: primary on power, ink when coasting, warning under braking. So the braking points of the last corner curl toward you, while a dashed 1.5 s predicted arc leads ahead. The performance bar's end cap shows the same phase colour live. At the limiter only the diamond strip strobes, and the lens rim holds a steady `shift`.
- **Telemetry:** world position x/y/z (bytes 244..255) → 120-point trail and elevation curtain (Y-up must be checked against the golden packets, 97% rule); throttle/brake at each trail sample → a phase byte stored with it; velocity + angular velocity y → κ = ω_y / max(v, 3 m/s) predicted arc; yaw → heading-up rotation; pitch/roll → map camera tilt ±3°; rpm/idle/max + shift → performance bar and diamonds; gear → lens glyph; speed → light numerals; power, torque, boost → shy tiles; lateral G → reflection band and light azimuth. There is no map or navigation data: the map is only the driven trail, labelled TRAIL, never NAV.
- **References:** Mercedes MBUX Hyperscreen and CLA Superscreen (Unity real-time 3D scene); BMW Panoramic iDrive black print band; Apple CarPlay Ultra thick-bar parametric themes; glider flight-recorder 3D track replays
- **Phase 6 assets:** Shared MSDF atlases for Saira 200, Saira Condensed 700 and Titillium Web 600; an optional low-poly ego chevron glTF (procedural fallback); blue-noise dither tile; optional mono brand wordmark (≤220×44) for a logo zone at the band's lower right. Lens, rims and bar cells are procedural.
- **Risk:** Design: the 3D map is rank 4 but the most complex thing on screen, so it could out-shout gear and speed or be confused with the ADAS family's forward road ribbons. Fix: curtain ≤35% luminance except the head, no emissive, an orbit view of the past trail (ADAS shows the road ahead), and mask-dissolved viewport edges. Perf: a 240-triangle strip plus dashed arc rendered at half-res into a 640×180 target ≈0.05 ms; instanced bar cells; ≈0.2 ms total. It needs a fixed [u8; 120] phase ring beside fh6-core::trail, with no allocation.
