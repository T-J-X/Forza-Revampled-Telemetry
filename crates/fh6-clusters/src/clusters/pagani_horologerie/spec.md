**Category note.** The Pagani Huayra is in the game and the Utopia is not. Step 1 names the Pagani whose dials this dash recreates.

An atelier chronograph. Two Ø420 dials, speed on the left and tach on the right, sit inside a tonneau case drawn only as a 1 px polished chamfer (50% lit, 15% shadow) around true black. Between them, on X = 1920, a twin-disc grande-date window holds the gear. Clous-de-Paris guilloché shows only where the G-driven light rakes it. Rank 1: Saira Condensed 700 gear, cap 170, in the window, under 12 lume cartouches set in a straight bridge rail (34 × 800 px) that joins the dial crowns. Rank 2: tabular Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 96, low in the speed dial and 360 px from the gear. Rank 3: openworked primary-colour hands over Barlow Semi Condensed 500 numerals; the tach bezel is set per car so the shift rpm sits at 12. Rank 4: twin retrograde pedal fans under the window. Rank 5: the machined chamfers, and the crest under the tach's 6.

- **Signature moment:** Grande-date jump. On a gear change the units disc jumps behind the window like an outsize date at midnight; the tens disc makes 10 a true two-digit numeral. The old numeral leaves 24 px upward under a 1 px window-edge shadow. The new one rises from 1.12 on the fast spring, and the window's polished frame takes the 1-frame 60% rim flash. At the limiter only the cartouche rail strobes. The steady cue is the tach bezel's 12 o'clock cartouche, which fills with the shift colour and stays lit. On a car change, and only then, the bezel ratchets round (24 detents, 600 ms) to put the new car's shift rpm under 12. The retrograde pedal hands fly back to zero on a critically damped snap spring.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the skeleton tach hand (needle spring with feed-forward) and the cartouche thresholds. CarOrdinal changes plus the car-profile shift rpm drive the bezel rotation. speed drives the speed hand and the tabular digits. gear drives the date discs, with R in warning and N at 72% ink. throttle and brake drive the retrograde fans (go / warning). Lateral G drives the light azimuth over the guilloché and chamfers, and the parallax (window 0, dials 2, case 4 px). fuel drives a small, shy power-reserve arc at 9 o'clock on the speed dial (rank 5, no glow). boost drives an optional secondary hand, shown only for turbo cars. fuel is not yet decoded by fh6-core; verify it against the golden packets.
- **References:** Pagani Utopia analog dials with exposed movement parts and a small screen between them; A. Lange & Söhne outsize-date (twin-disc big date) window; Breguet clous-de-Paris guilloché; retrograde complications
- **Phase 6 assets:** Clous-de-Paris normal map (P ≈ 12 px) and barleycorn (N ≈ 24), baked with mips; black anodised aluminium albedo/roughness for the case chamfer; a polished-steel matcap for the skeleton hands; the brand-crest dial variant. The date-disc numerals are MSDF, not textures. Procedural fallbacks: an analytic guilloché line field and a gradient chamfer.
- **Risk:** v1 drew an opaque navy capsule with gold outlines, a tiny gear and fake sub-dials, so it read as a flat sticker. The case must stay a 1 px chamfer around black, never a plate. Two equal dials can split attention away from a narrow window. Keep the window ≥220 px wide with the gear cap ≥150, keep the dial contrast below the gear's, and hold guilloché at ≤14 alpha with a mip bias to stop WRGB moiré. Performance: two normal-mapped Ø420 faces (~0.28 Mpx) with one fetch per pixel, est. 0.2 ms.
