# Kinetic Liquid Metal (`KineticLiquidMetal`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 27 (★ KineticLiquidMetal — build #7 · cockpit) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Fluid / Generative (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · ★ pattern-setter · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 7

Sealed borosilicate, not a drawing: a 240° glass channel (R 225, bore 40, open at the bottom) wraps a domed ampoule (R 140) at (0,−20), and the live mercury column inside is the tach (rank 3), its meniscus the exact reading. Mercury is an SDF smooth-union surface, normals from a 1D height field, shaded by `liquidMetal` env bands that rotate with the G-light, tinted 0.18 toward primary. Gear: Exo 2 800, cap 200, floating above a mercury pool tilting on `fluidSlosh`. Eleven Ø26 droplet beads (≤14 px sag) form the shift strip; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 112) fills the bottom gap above a 520×28 G spirit-level vial. Glass alpha 28–44, 1 px rims, nothing filled on black.

- **Signature moment:** Limiter (family signature): the meniscus swells and warms steadily to the `shift` hue (never flashes) while droplets pinch off the column tip at 5 Hz, run down a 1 px capillary and merge into the ampoule pool by smooth union (k 10 px). The pool level visibly rises the longer you sit on the limiter, then drains back over 300 ms after the shift. Only the bead strip strobes at 10 Hz. Upshift flourish: the old glyph sinks into the pool (0.92, out in 50 ms) and the new one surfaces through the meniscus from 1.12 with the 24 px upward slide on the `fast` spring. The surface closes over it with one under-damped slosh.
- **Telemetry:** current/idle/max rpm → mercury column length on the `ribbon` spring with velocity feed-forward (the meniscus tip is exact). Per-car, per-gear thresholds → 11 beads, outside-in, 0 ms attack, 120 rpm off-hysteresis. gear → ampoule glyph plus an upshift slosh impulse. speed → Exo 2 tabular field with ghost 8s. gLat/gLong → pool tilt atan(−gLat/(1+/gLong/)) on `fluidSlosh`, vial slug position (gLat) and slug length (gLong), and the light azimuth that rotates the env bands. throttle/brake → twin test tubes at x −560 (`go` / `warning`). Car slip angle → right micro-readout, stepping to `caution` above 8°. Surface rumble (a packet field the decoder does not expose yet) and suspension-travel velocity → ripple impulses on the column that decay flat. The Phase 8 ω = rpm·0.08 idle ripple is dropped, so idle is still.
- **References:** Family hero frames in docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/ (channel, ampoule, beads); laboratory mercury manometers and clinical thermometers; the liquid-metal register of Terminator 2 (1991), material only; the Lexus LFA meter (an instrument faster than any mechanism).
- **Phase 6 assets:** Liquid-metal flipbook (64 frames, 512² atlas) with matching flow and normal maps as the budget fallback. A 256×1 HDR studio env-band LUT baked in Blender (procedural fallback: the token `liquidMetal` env bands). A 128² blue-noise tile for low-alpha glass gradients. Exo 2, Titillium Web and Barlow Semi Condensed MSDF atlases. Brand crest dial variant etched on the lower-left channel cap (the logo zone).
- **Risk:** The hero's bead arc is about 90 px tall at 4K, which breaks the ≤42 px strobe strip: flatten it to ≤14 px sag (40 px band). Mercury reflections around the gear can fail the blur gate: add a feathered dimming pad (alpha ≤140×(1−scrim)) and hold the pool below the glyph baseline. The v1 build shipped a white full-canvas wash with no readable gear; nothing may fill black. Perf: the column and pool are 1D shallow-water height fields (128 + 64 cells) on one tiny compute dispatch, drawn by one SDF pass clipped to about 700×600, roughly 0.06 ms. Switch to the flipbook if bench-render exceeds 0.3 ms.
