**The Lorenz butterfly, with the engine's revs as its one control.** Lorenz's three equations of 1963 have a parameter ρ. Below 1 everything falls to a single point. Above 1 two resting points appear and move apart as ρ rises, and near 24.74 they lose their hold and the motion becomes the butterfly, the best known picture of chaos. Here ρ is 28 × rpm ÷ shift rpm, so the engine takes the figure through all of it: a still point with the engine off, two calm spirals in the middle of the range, and the butterfly over the last 11.7% of the revs before the shift point. Step 1 takes the equations and their constants from a textbook source and checks them against the maths table.

Starting layout, drawn in the x–z plane of the equations, seen with depth, 30 px for each unit across and 8.9 px for each unit up, with the origin at (0, +215). Rank 3: the two resting points are the tach. They sit at x = ±√(β(ρ − 1)), z = ρ − 1, so both ride the parabola z = x² ÷ β, and their height is linear in the revs: each is a bright eye on a ticked parabola that runs from the origin up to (±255, −25) at the shift point, with Barlow Semi Condensed numerals on the left limb. Rank 4: the figure, two glowing trails, one from each side. Rank 1: the gear (Exo 2 800, cap 150) on a true-black pad, Ø200 at (0, −40), the butterfly's body, and ten lamps on a straight line at y −296 (760 × 28). Rank 2: tabular speed (Exo 2 300, cap 84) on a pad under the gear at (0, +130). The hood view uses 40 px across and 7 px up.

- **Signature moment:** Lift-off. Two points, exact mirror images of each other, move by Lorenz's equations, and each leaves a glowing trail. Their clock is the distance driven, one unit of the model's time for every 50 m, with a floor of half a unit per second so that they settle when the car stands. Below ρ = 24.74 every path ends in an eye: when the revs change the eyes move, the points chase them round in spirals, and at steady revs each point comes to rest in its eye while its trail shrinks into it, so a steady cruise is two still lights. As the revs near the shift window the spirals take more and more turns to settle, and past ρ = 24.74 they never do: the points loop round both eyes without repeating, and the trails draw the butterfly for as long as the revs stay there. On an upshift the revs fall, the eyes catch the points again and the wings fold back into spirals. On the limiter only the lamps strobe, and both eyes hold a steady `shift` colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm and the car's shift rpm for the gear → ρ, and with it the eyes, the figure, the lamps and the limiter; speed and the timestamp → distance, the model's clock; gear → the numeral; speed → the tabular speed; lateral acceleration → the view turns about the vertical axis by 12° for each g, which shows the figure's depth; throttle → the brightness of the curves. Every one of these is a verified field.
- **References:** Lorenz's 1963 paper and the standard values σ = 10, β = 8 ÷ 3; the bifurcations of the Lorenz system as a textbook gives them; `HarmonographDrive` in this catalog, the other curve-drawing dash, which this one must not be mistaken for
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural: both points are integrated on the CPU (fourth-order Runge–Kutta, steps of 0.005 units, a few steps a frame), and each trail is a fixed ring of 1,000 samples, the last 20 units of the model's time, drawn as line chunks sorted far to near. The eyes and the parabola come from the closed form, never from the integration. ComfyUI: a soft glow sprite for the eyes and a fine grain for the black pads' rims. Blender: none. Penpot: the board at ρ = 0.5, 10, 20 and 28. MSDF atlases for Exo 2 300 and 800 and Barlow Semi Condensed 500.
- **Risk:** The figure is busiest exactly when the driver must shift: the lamps and the gear carry that moment, no part of the figure is brighter than 30% of the gear, and its change is the message, not something to be read. Two dots on a parabola can look empty in the middle of the range: the spirals round the eyes are the figure there, and the direction check looks at ρ = 10 and ρ = 20 first. A trail redrawn from scratch would shimmer in chaos, which is why the figure is a trail with a clock and never a fresh curve each frame; the trails are soft lines of at least 2 px. Perf: a few integration steps a frame and 250 line chunks, est. 0.2 ms.
