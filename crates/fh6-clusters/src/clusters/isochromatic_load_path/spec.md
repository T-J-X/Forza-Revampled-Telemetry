# Isochromatic Load Path (`IsochromaticLoadPath`): brief

- **Family:** Fluid / Generative (Design Council 3C), spec [docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/fluid-generative.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/fluid-generative/)
- **Kind:** new · **View:** cockpit · **Build order:** 36

A clear polycarbonate single-seater seen from above, set between crossed circular polarisers: unstressed material is optically black (off pixels), so light exists only where the car is loaded. Its front wing (1100×60, y −235) is the tach: a point-load puck rides it to the rpm position (rank 3, emissive) while a fan of fringes radiates beneath. Thirteen pills on the wing's leading edge form the shift strip (y −292). Gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200) sits in the tub at (0,−30) on a dimming pad; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 84) on the engine cover at (0,+165). Contact patches at (±400,−170) and (±400,+175) show load as monochrome isochromatics, sin²(πN) in primary only; chassis drawn in 1 px rims.

- **Signature moment:** Load transfer becomes light. Trail-brake into a corner and the outside-front contact patch blooms into 6–8 tight concentric fringe loops (Flamant circles tangent at the patch) while the unloaded inside-rear goes black. Throttle out of a hairpin lights the driven axle's patches. Lateral G rotates the analyser ±10°, so dark isoclinic bands sweep across the chassis as you turn, which is a real polariscope effect. Gear change: the wing's point load releases, and its fringe fan collapses and re-forms at the new rpm on the `snap` spring while the puck moves instantly. Limiter: the wing fringes reach order 6 and freeze in a steady `shift` tint; only the pill strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** normalized_suspension_travel FL/FR/RL/RR (decoded) plus gLat/gLong load transfer → point-load magnitude at each contact patch (`fast` spring, 2% deadband). drivetrain_type → which axle shows torque-reaction fringes under throttle (RWD rear, FWD front, AWD both), scaled by throttle. Per-wheel combined slip (packet field, decoder extension needed; verify against golden packets) and tyre slip angles → patch smear direction, with `caution`/`warning` outline steps at 0.08/0.18. rpm → puck position (`ribbon` spring with feed-forward) and wing fringe order N ∝ rpm/shiftRpm (≤6). Per-gear thresholds → pills, outside-in. gear → tub glyph. speed → tabular. gLat → analyser angle on the `light` spring.
- **References:** Photoelastic stress analysis (Brewster, 1816; Frocht, Photoelasticity, 1941); Flamant's half-plane point-load solution (1892), whose isochromatics are circles tangent at the load; engineering polariscope demonstrations of loaded discs and hooks; photoelastic granular force-chain experiments.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only: a closed-form superposition of four Flamant point-load stress tensors per pixel, principal stress difference → fringe order N → sin²(πN). This is art-correct, not FEA. Optional car-class plan silhouettes as SVG through resvg (single-seater, GT, SUV, truck) with an SDF capsule-and-box fallback. 128² blue-noise for low-alpha fringe tails. Exo 2 MSDF.
- **Risk:** Fringe moiré and shimmer on the WRGB subpixels at high N: cap N at 8, antialias each fringe with fwidth, and fade fringes thinner than 3 px. Noisy suspension data would make the loops boil; the spring and deadband keep idle still. A plan-view car can read as a toy, so draw it with 1 px rims only, at ≤20% luminance, with no fills. Perf: four tensor sums per pixel over two clips (about 0.25 MP), one pass, about 0.03 ms, the cheapest live visual in the family.
