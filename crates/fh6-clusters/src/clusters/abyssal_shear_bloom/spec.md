A black lagoon, dark until disturbed: light is emitted only where water shears, like dinoflagellates. Two mirrored wake channels sweep up and out from the gear like gull wings (each 600×180, 1 px glass rims at alpha 28); a forcing jet runs centre-out in both to the rpm point, its tip the exact reading (rank 3, emissive), while shear along its flanks lights plankton in primary. Gear (Exo 2 800, cap 200) in a clear lens at (0,−80) on a dimming pad; speed (Exo 2 300, cap 112) at (0,+95). Thirteen 12×30 photophore lozenges form the shift strip at y −296. Beneath, a 900×80 lagoon holds four wheel paddles stirred by tyre slip; its light never blooms.

- **Signature moment:** Shear is the only light source. Hold a constant speed on smooth tarmac and the lagoon goes black while the wings hold a steady glowing jet. Slide a tyre and its paddle tears a glowing vortex pair through the lagoon, which spirals and fades over 400 ms, so you see slip at the corner where it happens. Each upshift fires one pressure ring outward from the gear lens through both wings: a single expanding band of plankton light. Limiter: the jet tips curl into steady glowing eddies at the wing ends in `shift`; only the photophore strip strobes.
- **Telemetry:** rpm → jet length centre-out in both wings (exact tip, `ribbon` spring with feed-forward). throttle → jet velocity and flank-shear brightness. Per-gear thresholds → photophores, outside-in. gear → upshift pressure ring (a single splat impulse). speed → tabular. Per-wheel combined slip (decoder extension needed) and tyre slip angles (decoded) → paddle stirring force at four footprint positions. Surface rumble (decoder extension) → small stir impulses at the paddles. Puddle depth (decoder extension) → plankton density in 3 steps (a wet track gives a brighter sea). gLat/gLong → lagoon body force (slosh). All forcing at zero → the lagoon dissipates to black. The plankton are a synthetic visual, not sensor data.
- **References:** Noctiluca scintillans 'blue tears' bioluminescent coastlines; Pavel Dobryakov's MIT-licensed WebGL Fluid Simulation (solver defaults and relief shading); teamLab's interactive light-on-water installations; deep-sea photophore anatomy for the shift lozenges.
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural: a stable-fluids solver clipped to two 600×180 wing rects and a 900×80 lagoon (sim 160×48 and 224×20, RG16F velocity, R16F emission at half resolution, warm-started pressure). 128² blue-noise for plankton sparkle seeding. Optional 32-frame sparkle-and-eddy flipbook as the budget fallback. Exo 2 MSDF.
- **Risk:** The 2D solver is the costliest item in the family. Clip every pass to the three rects (about 0.3 MP at full resolution), run 12 warm-started Jacobi iterations at sim resolution and emission at half resolution: about 0.12–0.18 ms. Switch to the flipbook if bench-render exceeds 0.3 ms. Lagoon light is rank 4 and must never feed the emissive buffer. The sea must settle to black: dissipation exp(−d·dt), emission decay τ 250 ms, and a strain threshold S0 so numerical noise never sparkles at idle.
