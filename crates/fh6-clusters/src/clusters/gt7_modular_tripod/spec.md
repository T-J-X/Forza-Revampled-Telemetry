# GT7 Modular Tripod (`Gt7ModularTripod`): brief

Copied from [CATALOG.md](../../../../../program/phases/07-cluster-factory/CATALOG.md) row 14 (Gt7ModularTripod — build #17 · cockpit+hood) by the Phase 7 setup chat. The catalog stays the source of truth; this copy is what the cluster chat builds against.

- **Family:** Race Dash (Design Council 3B), spec [docs/design/families/race-dash.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/race-dash.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/race-dash/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/race-dash/)
- **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** cockpit+hood · **Build order:** 17

Three machined black-anodised aluminium capsules: a 600×480 centre module and two 360×280 satellites yawed 9° toward the driver by a homography and set one depth layer back. Each capsule is a thin spun-grain ring with a 3 px polished 45° chamfer lit by `light`, around a true-black cavity. Rank 1: one floating 36 px strip of 11 pods (3+5+3) bridging all three tops, plus the `race` 800 gear at cap 228 centre. Rank 2: speed, cap 112, beneath it. Rank 3: a 60-segment arch tach cresting over the gear. Rank 4: each satellite is a three-faced prism of pages (left TYRES / G / INPUTS, right LAP / POWER / TRIP), with two shy lamps on its outer edge.

- **Signature moment:** Prism page turn: an event rolls a satellite's triangular prism 120° about its horizontal axis on the `default` spring (237 ms); the two visible faces foreshorten by cos θ and shade by dot(n, L) from the cluster light, like a trackside three-sided rotating billboard. Brake >0.5 with combined slip flips the left to TYRES; a completed lap flips the right to LAP; at most one turn per 2.5 s, never on the centre module. The outside-in fill jumps the two capsule gaps to land on the centre pod; at the limiter the strip strobes while the arch tach's last 6 segments hold a steady `shift`.
- **Telemetry:** rpm + car-profile thresholds → 11-pod strip, strobe and arch tach (segments numbered from engine_max_rpm); gear → centre glyph; speed_ms → speed; tyre temps ×4 + combined slip → TYRES face; acceleration_x/z → G face ball (felt force, −acceleration) and light/parallax; accel/brake/clutch/hand_brake → INPUTS face bars; lap fields + synthesised distance-based delta → LAP face; power_watts, torque, boost_psi → POWER face; fuel + distance → TRIP face; brake, slip and the lap-number edge → page-turn events; lamps LIM, FUEL and synthesised TC/ABS.
- **References:** Bugatti Tourbillon (one hero centre dial with two satellites); three-sided rotating trivision billboards; Bosch DDU event-triggered page changes; Pagani Utopia black anodised machined cluster
- **Phase 6 assets:** Spun-grain (tangential) anisotropy map, strength in R and rotation in G, for the capsule rings, plus a 45° chamfer normal profile; procedural anisotropic fallback; MSDF Saira Condensed 700/800 and Titillium Web 600; prism faces carry live vector content, no bitmaps.
- **Risk:** Design: three capsules read as three gadgets, and page turns tug the periphery. Avoid: one shared pod strip and one light tie the trio; satellites carry only ranks 4–5; turns are event-only and rate-limited; the zen tier hides the satellites. Perf: prism faces are live vectors under a vertical scale (no render-to-texture), the homography runs in the vertex stage, rings are one anisotropic pass: ≈0.2 ms. Speed stays in the centre module, so it is always within 420 px of the gear. Hood [4]: satellites move to ±700 px at 6° yaw.
