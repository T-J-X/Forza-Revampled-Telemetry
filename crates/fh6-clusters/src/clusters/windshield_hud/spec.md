- **Category:** Apple Vision · **Target:** `concept` · **Kind:** rebuild · **View:** raised HUD
- **Family:** ADAS & HUD (Design Council 3D), spec [docs/design/families/adas-hud.md](../../../../../docs/design/families/adas-hud.md), hero frames [docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/](../../../../../docs/design/heroes/adas-hud/)

**Category note.** The category trait is the virtual image placed 10 m out in the world. The collimated strokes stay; no glass panes.

Civil AR-HUD virtual image at (1920,1595), focused 10 m out: no plate, only a feathered #000 dimming pad (alpha ≤150, 48 px feather) hugging the symbology so it reads against bright sky. Vertical stack on X=1920: Z1 crown of 21 slender 8×22 px staves on a 16 px-sag arc spanning 960 px; beneath, a full-width 1 px horizon whose 3 px rpm core (rank 3) grows centre-out. Gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 160) sits in a 1 px four-tick reticle, speed beneath in B612 Mono (cap 96), then Z4: a flat perspective map ribbon with the trail behind and a dashed κ prediction 3 s ahead, stepped accent/caution/warning braking zones and an ego chevron. Near symbols take ±4 px G parallax; the ribbon stays fixed.

- **Signature moment:** The crown closes. Staves fill outside-in on per-gear thresholds while the horizon rpm core grows centre-out; at the shift rpm the two meet at both ends, the crown becomes one closed arc in the shift colour, and the reticle's four ticks snap 8 px inward. At the limiter only the 38 px-tall stave row strobes 10 Hz; the horizon and reticle hold steady. In a drift the ribbon's ego chevron yaws to the nose heading while a 2 px slip arrow continues along the velocity, with β labelled in B612 Mono.
- **Telemetry:** rpm → staves and horizon core; gear → reticle; speed → readout and lookahead length (3 s × v); world position x/z (bytes 244..255) → 120-point trail; brake sampled into each trail node → stepped zones (accent < 20%, caution < 60%, warning ≥ 60%); angular velocity y / max(v, 3 m/s) → κ arc ahead; local accel z + speed → a predicted braking zone ahead at v²/(2/a/); local velocity x/z → β slip arrow; roll → ribbon counter-roll so it stays level; lateral G → light azimuth and parallax. There is no map or route in the packet: the road ahead is a constant-curvature guess, drawn dashed and faded out by 1.5 s so it never reads as map data.
- **References:** BMW Neue Klasse Panoramic Vision plus 3D HUD (upper alert layer over a black band); Mercedes S-Class AR-HUD (10°×5° window, image at about 10 m); Garmin G1000 synthetic-vision flight-path symbology; F/A-18 collimated stroke HUD
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (SDF staves, reticle and horizon, a ribbon triangle strip); MSDF atlases B612 Mono, Saira Condensed 700, Titillium Web 600; the shared blue-noise tile to dither the dimming-pad feather
- **Risk:** It floats over the windscreen, so contrast against sky and road is the failure mode (v1 even drew an opaque white box). The dimming pad must let the gear pass 7:1 and the speed 4.5:1 in the blur gate at its alpha cap; lit area stays ≤8% of the 1280×480 window, nothing below y +230 so the sightline stays clear, and the user height offset is honoured. `fh6-core::trail` stores only x/z today, so it needs a brake byte per node in its fixed array (no allocation). Cost: a ≈400-triangle ribbon plus SDF strokes, <0.1 ms.
