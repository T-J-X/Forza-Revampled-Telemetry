**A field of jewelled seeds set out as a sunflower sets them.** Seed number n sits at the distance c√n from the middle and at the angle n × 137.508°, the golden angle. That one rule packs the seeds evenly and makes the spirals of a sunflower head appear, in counts that are always Fibonacci numbers. The packing is perfect only at that exact angle: turn it by a twentieth of a degree and the seeds line up in spokes that curl into arms. Here the car turns the angle. Driving straight, the field is a calm sunflower; in a corner it gathers into arms. Step 1 takes the model (Vogel, 1979) from a source and checks the values in the maths table.

Starting layout: a wide field of seeds over OLED black, cut to an ellipse 1100 × 400 centred at (0, −10), with c = 20 px, which puts about 280 seeds in view. Each seed is a domed bead, Ø26, lit from under. Rank 1: the gear (Exo 2 800, cap 160) on a true-black core, Ø200, in the middle of the field, and ten lamps on a straight line at y −296 (760 × 28), filling outside-in. Rank 2: tabular speed (Exo 2 300, cap 90) on a black pad, 250 × 130 at (+330, −10). The same pad mirrored at (−330, −10) shows power. Rank 3: a thin light bar under the field, 1100 px long at y +215, lit from the left, with ticks and Barlow Semi Condensed numerals. Rank 4: the field. Seeds light from the middle outward in the order of their numbers, as an echo of the bar and never the reading. The hood view uses an ellipse of 1500 × 300.

- **Signature moment:** The arms. The angle between one seed and the next is the golden angle plus 0.25° for each g of lateral force. At rest the field is a sunflower head: two families of spirals cross evenly and neither leads. In a corner every seed slides round its own circle and the field gathers into spokes that curl into arms. The fractions that come closest to the golden angle fall on alternate sides of it, so the two directions look different: pushed one way the seeds stand in 89 straight spokes at 0.08 g and in 34 at 0.56 g, pushed the other way in 55 at 0.21 g and in 21 at 1.46 g, and between those the spokes curl. Back on the straight the arms dissolve into the sunflower. Braking and accelerating breathe the field: c changes by 6% for each g. At steady speed on a straight road nothing moves. On the limiter only the lamps strobe, and the lit seeds hold a steady `shift` colour.
- **Telemetry:** rpm, idle and max with the car's per-gear thresholds → the light bar, the count of lit seeds, the lamps and the limiter; gear → the numeral; speed → the tabular speed; power → the left pad, with the peak of the current pull held; lateral acceleration → the turn of the angle; longitudinal acceleration → the spacing c; throttle → the brightness of the lit seeds. Every one of these is a verified field.
- **References:** Vogel's model of the sunflower head (1979) and the golden angle; Fibonacci spirals in plants; jewelled and faceted dial textures in watchmaking; `CymaticResonanceField` in this catalog for a field of many small parts
- **Phase 6 assets:** Blender: one bead as a domed crystal with a faceted underside, rendered at 4K as a sprite with its normal map and the light variants the G-swing needs; the same sprite serves every seed. ComfyUI: the bead's inner sparkle as a labelled grain map, and the dim studio it reflects. Penpot: the board with the field at rest and at 0.5 g and 1 g. Under the colour law a seed's hue may follow its place in one spiral family (its number modulo 21), through the palette's ramp. MSDF atlases for Exo 2 300 and 800 and Barlow Semi Condensed 500.
- **Risk:** Hundreds of bright beads would swamp the gear: unlit seeds sit at 6% and lit ones at no more than 35% of the gear, and the core and the pads are true black. A field that swirls in every corner can distract: the arms form over 300 ms on a spring and never spin on their own. Seeds must not flicker as the lit edge passes them: each fades up over 80 ms. Perf: about 280 instanced sprites, one bar and ten lamps, est. 0.15 ms.
