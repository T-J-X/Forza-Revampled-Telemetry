**Category note.** No Bugatti is in the game's car list, so no car selects this dash by itself: the driver picks it in the OSD, and its logo zone shows the driven car's logo.

One Ø520 skeleton dial on X = 1920, with no satellites. The movement spills past the dial as openwork bridges reaching 300 px each side on the −4 px layer. They are linearly brushed at ≤20% luminance, with anglage bands that catch the G-driven light. Rank 1: a Ø210 tourbillon cage turns at the dial centre around a gear numeral that stays upright and still (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170). Eleven clear jewels set in the top bridge form the shift strip, 30 × 740 px. Rank 2: tabular Saira Condensed 600 speed, cap 90, in an aperture under the cage. Rank 3: there is no centre hand. A lume-tipped peripheral carriage rides a rail round the dial edge over Barlow Semi Condensed numerals and reaches 12 at the shift rpm. Rank 4: the road drives the movement, so wheelspin visibly overspeeds the cage against a ground-speed index. Rank 5: jewel bearings tinted with the palette accent, and the crest at 6.

- **Signature moment:** Escapement beat. The cage is geared to the driven wheels: it turns with the road and stops dead at a standstill, so idle is perfectly still. On each gear change it takes one visible beat, a 50 ms hold then a 30° jump on the fast spring, as the new numeral rises inside it. The numeral is counter-rotated every frame so it never tilts. At the limiter only the 11-jewel bridge strip strobes. The carriage's lume tip, sitting just past 12, burns a steady shift colour with a 6 px halo.
- **Telemetry:** rpm drives the peripheral carriage (needle spring with feed-forward) and the jewel thresholds; the car-profile shift rpm puts the carriage at 12 (fallback: limiter × 0.97). The driven-wheel rotation speeds, chosen by DrivetrainType (FWD, RWD or AWD mean), drive the cage and gear-train angle at wheel rps / 16. speed drives the tabular digits and the ground-speed index ring; wheelspin shows as the cage overspeeding the ring. gear drives the cage numeral. boost and fuel feed two shy apertures in the side bridges (rank 5). Lateral G drives the light on the anglage and the parallax (cage 0, dial 2, bridges 4 px). Wheel rotation speed and fuel are not yet decoded by fh6-core; add them and verify them against tests/golden/packets.
- **References:** Bugatti Tourbillon instrument cluster (Concepto-built, 600+ parts, centre dial with small digital speed and gear); flying-tourbillon haute horlogerie; skeletonised calibres with anglage and Côtes de Genève; peripheral-hand watch architectures
- **Phase 6 assets:** A glTF tourbillon cage, a 5-wheel gear train and bridges (≤15k tris) from tools/asset-forge, with an SDF recipe as the fallback. Also: a linear-brushed steel albedo with an anglage normal map; a Côtes de Genève stripe normal map for the bridges; a jewel-lens matcap tinted at runtime from the palette; the brand-crest dial variant.
- **Risk:** v1 drew a grey plate with a gear smaller than its speed and a cog icon. The skeleton can also become busy grey structure that fights the gear and wears the OLED. Keep the movement layer at ≤20% luminance and ≤12% of the envelope's lit area, with no bloom and no live depth of field. The jewels take the palette accent instead of ruby red so red never becomes structure. Performance: a ≤15k-tri mesh pass with MSAA, drawn only into the envelope-clipped target, est. 0.1 ms; whole cluster est. 0.3 ms.
