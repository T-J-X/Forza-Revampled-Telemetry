**Inspired by Apple's CarPlay Ultra** (TJX, 2026-11-28: "apple vision style, carplay inspired"). Written without a web check, because the search budget had run out: everything below about the real display is recalled, and step 1 confirms it from Apple's and Aston Martin's own pages before anything is drawn. Recalled: CarPlay Ultra arrived in May 2025, first in Aston Martin cars; the phone draws every driver screen, the instrument cluster included; the driver picks one of several cluster layouts and colour themes made with the car maker, among them two round dials with content between them and a layout that gives most of the screen to the map; widgets and live information sit between the dials; and since iOS 26 CarPlay wears Apple's Liquid Glass. Not established: the dials' geometry (sweep, ticks, numerals, needle or arc), where the gear and the speed sit in each layout, the names of the layouts, and how the real cluster marks the shift point. The dash takes the two-dial layout with glass widgets between the dials, and adds one part the real display does not have: the island.

Starting layout, at three depths. Far: two round dials on true black, Ø400 at (±390, −10). The left dial is the speed, with its tabular digits (Saira 300, cap 90) at its centre, 391 px from the gear (rank 2). The right dial is the rev counter (rank 3): a thin tick ring, numerals inside it and a lit arc. Middle: a clear glass squircle, 440 × 300 at (0, −40), that overlaps the inner edge of both dials by 30 px and holds the gear (Saira Condensed 700, cap 170); under it two glass widgets, 200 × 110 at (±110, +175). Near: the island, a glass capsule 40 px tall at y −290 over ten lamp seats that span 760 px (rank 1). Rank 4: the left widget holds three rings in the manner of Apple's activity rings (power as a share of the highest power seen this session, boost, brake), and the right widget holds a G-dot. Rank 5: Titillium Web labels on the dials, and the logo zone.

- **Signature moment:** The island. At rest the capsule is a pill 200 px wide round the two centre seats, and the other eight seats show as dim dots, so the strip's place never moves. As the revs enter the shift window the glass stretches outward from the centre, one seat a side at a time, and each seat lights as the glass reaches it; at the shift point the island is 800 px wide and all ten are lit. It moves on a critically damped spring, x(t) = x_T + (x₀ − x_T)(1 + ωt)e^(−ωt) with ω = 14 rad/s, so it never overshoots and is within 5% after 0.34 s. On an upshift it lets go and returns to the pill in one motion. On the limiter only the lamps inside the island strobe, and the arcs of both dials hold a steady `shift` colour. Lateral G shears the three depths by 0, 2 and 4 px per g, and the dial ticks seen through the rim of the middle pane are refracted by it.
- **Telemetry:** speed → the left dial's arc and digits; rpm, idle and max with the car's per-gear thresholds → the right dial, the island and the limiter; gear → the numeral in the pane; power → the outer ring, as a share of the session's highest power (one stored `f32`); boost → the middle ring (it shows throttle, and its label says so, while boost has stayed under 0.05 bar since the start); brake → the inner ring; acceleration → the G-dot on the `gBall` spring and the parallax of the three depths. Every one of these is a verified field. No map, no media and no phone data: the game sends none.
