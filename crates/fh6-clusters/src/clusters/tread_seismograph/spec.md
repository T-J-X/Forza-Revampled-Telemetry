The car writes the road. A low chase camera looks forward over a 1 px chassis footprint, and from each contact patch a strip-chart ribbon runs back toward the viewer: a 256-sample seismograph of what that wheel felt. Height is suspension travel (≤40 px), fine teeth are surface rumble, a mirror-glass segment with a fresnel rim is puddle depth, and lateral smear is slip angle, so a slide literally draws sideways. Z1: ten 48×14 px stylus pods at y −292, filling outside-in. Gear centre (Saira Condensed 800, cap 170), speed left (Saira Light, cap 112). Rank 3 is a horizontal rpm scale whose glowing stylus head trails a 1.5 s ink trace that scrolls up like chart paper. Tracks are hairline neutral ink; colour appears only for slip and lock.

- **Signature moment:** The kerb strike. A rumble-strip hit spikes only the touched wheels' tracks in a sharp burst of teeth (inkMuted, no glow) that scrolls back at road speed. Wheelspin widens the driven tracks into caution slip bands; lock-up turns the front pair into flat warning dashes. At the shift rpm the stylus reaches the shift mark and every pod takes the shift colour. At the limiter only the pod strip strobes 10 Hz.
- **Telemetry:** Normalised suspension travel ×4 → track height; surface rumble ×4 → teeth; puddle depth ×4 → glass segments; tyre slip angle ×4 → lateral smear; slip ratio ×4 → caution spin bands and warning lock dashes; tyre temps ×4 → contact-patch thermal step, shown only outside the optimal window; speed → scroll rate (one sample per 0.5 m, so the tracks are still when stopped); rpm → pods, stylus and trace; gear and speed → hero readouts. All per-wheel data is real; nothing is synthesised.
- **References:** Seismograph and polygraph strip-chart recorders; Harold Craft's 1970 stacked plots of pulsar CP 1919 (the ridgeline as a data form); MoTeC i2 suspension and kerb traces used by race engineers; Mercedes Magic Body Control road-surface scanning
- **Phase 6 assets:** Procedural only (4 ring-buffer triangle strips of 256 samples, updated in place by ring offset; SDF footprint, pods and stylus; a small fresnel glass segment); MSDF atlases Saira Condensed 800, Saira Light, Titillium Web 600
- **Risk:** Four busy traces could steal attention from gear and speed. Clamp amplitude at 40 px, keep 2 px hairline weight and alpha ≤140, never bloom the tracks, and fade them over the last 40% of their length; at idle they are flat and still. Rumble, puddle depth and wheel rotation speed are not decoded yet: add them and verify against tests/golden/packets (97% rule), or ship with suspension and slip only. GPU <0.05 ms (≈2k vertices rewritten in place, no reallocation).
